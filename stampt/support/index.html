Frequently Asked Questions

How do I capture a photo through a stamp?

Open Stampt, pick a stamp from the carousel, frame your shot through the viewfinder window, and tap the shutter. The photo lands inside the stamp's perforated edges automatically.

Can I edit a stamped photo afterwards?

Yes. From the album, tap any capture to re-frame, swap the stamp, adjust the effect intensity, or share. Originals are kept on-device so you can always re-stamp.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Every capture is processed and stored entirely on your iPhone. Stampt never uploads your photos to any server. No account required.

How do I save a stamped photo to my Photos library?

Tap the share button on any stamped photo and choose Save Image. Stampt will ask for one-time photo library write access if needed.

What does Pro unlock?

Pro unlocks the full vintage stamp catalog and removes capture limits. You can subscribe weekly, annually, or buy lifetime in the Paywall screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Stampt. From there you can cancel at any time.
