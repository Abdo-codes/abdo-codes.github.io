FAQ

Quick answers before you need support.

Does Wi-Fi QR upload my network password?

No. Saved networks stay on your device, passwords are stored in the iOS Keychain, and there is no account or server sync. The app counts anonymous usage events with Firebase Analytics, such as a QR code being created, but it never logs Wi-Fi names, passwords, QR contents, scanned text or sign text.

Can I print a guest Wi-Fi sign straight from the app?

Yes. Tap Print directly inside the guest sign sheet, or export a PDF to share. Classic, Code Only and 8-per-page Cut-out Cards are free; Wi-Fi QR Pro is a one-time unlock for 7 additional templates (Gallery Placard, Concierge Ledger, Signal Grid, Ink-Safe Editorial, Keycard, Olive Branch and Arrival Folio) across 4 × 6 in, 5 × 7 in, A4 and US Letter.

What is included for free vs. Wi-Fi QR Pro?

Free includes up to 2 saved networks, unlimited QR generation, full-screen bright QR mode, one-tap Copy Password and Send as Text, router sticker and photo scanning, direct printing, and 3 sign templates (Classic, Code Only and Cut-out Cards). Wi-Fi QR Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription that unlocks all 10 sign templates, Home and Lock Screen widgets, custom QR dot patterns, brand color and logo, 3 extra pass themes, unlimited saved networks and removing the app link from Classic signs.

Can I use it for an Airbnb or vacation rental?

Yes. Create a guest Wi-Fi QR code, add your rental or café name above the headline, pick a printable sign or 8 cut-out cards, and place it in a rental, guest room, office, café or shared space.

Is this a WiFi QR code template?

It replaces the template hunt for many hosts: enter or scan the network details once, then print or export a ready-to-scan sign instead of editing a generic Canva or Etsy template by hand. Read the full Wi-Fi QR vs Canva and Etsy templates comparison.

Does it work with older WEP routers and hidden networks?

Yes. Choose WPA/WPA2/WPA3, WEP, or No Password Required in the editor, and toggle Hidden Network when needed. The editor validates WEP keys (5 or 13 characters, or 10 or 26 hex digits) before you save.

Can I add a sign to a welcome binder or house manual?

Yes. Share the printable sign PDF or QR image and drop it into a house manual, digital guest guide, check-in message or printed welcome book.

Does it recover saved Wi-Fi passwords?

No. iOS does not let third-party apps read saved system Wi-Fi passwords. You enter or paste the password, scan a router sticker or printed note, or follow the built-in quick steps to find it in iOS Settings and Passwords.

Why does the app ask for location permission?

iOS requires location permission before an app can read the current Wi-Fi network name. Wi-Fi QR uses it only for that optional network-name shortcut and works without it.

How do Home & Lock Screen widgets work?

With Wi-Fi QR Pro, tap Pin to Widget on any saved network to display a scannable QR code in a small or medium Home Screen widget, or add a Lock Screen widget that opens your QR pass in one tap.
