Privacy

Your Wi-Fi details stay with you.

No account, no ads and no server sync. Saved networks stay on your device. Anonymous usage events help improve the app, and they never include your Wi-Fi details.

Saved networks Stored only on your device, so the app can make the QR code again.
Usage events Anonymous app events through Firebase Analytics, such as "QR code created". Never network names, passwords, QR contents, scanned text or sign text.
Location permission Used only when iOS requires it to read the current Wi-Fi network name.
Camera permission Used only when you choose to scan a router sticker or printed password.
Sharing QR images and sign PDFs leave the app only when you share, save or print them.

FAQ

Small answers before you need support.

Does Wi-Fi QR upload my network password?

No. Saved networks stay on your device, and there is no account or server sync. The app counts anonymous usage events with Firebase Analytics, such as a QR code being created, but it never logs Wi-Fi names, passwords, QR contents, scanned text or sign text.

Can I use it for an Airbnb or vacation rental?

Yes. Create a guest Wi-Fi QR code, customize a printable WiFi password sign, and place it in a rental, guest room, office, cafe or shared space.

Is this a WiFi QR code template?

It replaces the template hunt for many hosts: enter or scan the network details once, then export a ready-to-print sign instead of editing a generic Canva or Etsy template.

Can I print a guest Wi-Fi sign?

Yes. Pick one of six templates and a 4 x 6 in, 5 x 7 in, A4 or US Letter size, add your own title and footer, then print or share the PDF from the share sheet.

Does it work with older WEP routers?

Yes. Choose WEP in the Security picker. The editor checks that the key has 5 or 13 characters, or 10 or 26 hex digits, before you save.

Can I add it to a welcome binder or house manual?

Yes. Share the printable sign PDF or QR image and include it in a house manual, digital guest guide, check-in instructions or printed welcome book.

Does it recover saved Wi-Fi passwords?

No. iOS does not let the app read saved system Wi-Fi passwords. You enter the password or scan printed text that you can review.

Why does the app ask for location permission?

iOS requires location permission before an app can read the current Wi-Fi network name. Wi-Fi QR uses it only for that network-name shortcut.

Can I use it for hidden networks?

Yes. Add the network manually and turn on Hidden Network before you make the QR code or sign.
