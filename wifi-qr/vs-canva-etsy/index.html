Honest trade-offs

Where Canva, Etsy, or Apple Passwords is the better choice

An app built for fast guest Wi-Fi signs is not the right tool for every host.

Pick Canva when...

  • You already pay for Canva Pro to design your full welcome binder, checkout checklist, and local restaurant guide in one brand kit.
  • You want custom illustrations, property photos, or proprietary fonts on the Wi-Fi page.
  • Tip: generate a crisp vector QR SVG in our free browser generator and drop it into your Canva page without touching a sketchy QR site.

Pick Etsy physical signs when...

  • You want a laser-engraved wood or frosted acrylic plaque mounted on the wall and your guest Wi-Fi password never changes.
  • You prefer paying once for a physical piece of decor rather than framing a printed 4 x 6 or 5 x 7 card.

Pick Apple iOS 18 Passwords when...

  • One friend is standing next to you with an Android phone and you just want to flash the code on your screen once. See our iPhone to Android Wi-Fi guide.

Pick Wi-Fi QR when...

  • You want one screen that guarantees the QR code and printed password never drift apart.
  • You manage multiple guest rooms and want 8 cut-out cards on a single A4 or US Letter page.
  • You do not want your guest network password sent to third-party servers or locked behind a subscription.

FAQ

Questions hosts ask before switching

Why do Canva and Etsy WiFi signs break after a password change?

In a template, the QR image is decoupled from the text box. Hosts update the typed password and forget to regenerate and swap the pasted QR graphic, so guests who scan get an authentication error.

How much does an Etsy or Canva Airbnb WiFi sign cost in 2026?

Etsy digital templates usually cost $2 to $5 ($10 to $15 for bundles), physical acrylic signs cost $20 to $30+, and Canva Pro costs $18/month or $144/year. Wi-Fi QR is free with 3 included print layouts and an optional one-time Pro unlock.

Can I just use Apple's built-in Passwords app on iOS 18?

Yes for in-person screen sharing on networks your iPhone already joined. It does not export a printable guest sign or handle networks you have not joined.
