How it works

What happens when a guest scans your Wi-Fi QR code?

Your code stores one plain text string (WIFI:T:WPA;S:Network;P:Password;;) directly in the black-and-white pattern. iOS Camera and Android Camera parse that standard string locally and prompt the guest to tap Join without installing any app.

1

Static and offline forever

Unlike "dynamic QR" websites that break after a 14-day trial, this code contains no redirect URL. It works as long as your router password stays the same.

2

Special characters escaped safely

Backslashes, semicolons, colons, commas, and quotes in your network name or password are escaped per the ZXing/Apple Wi-Fi QR spec so scanning never drops characters.

3

Pair QR with readable text

FAQ

Wi-Fi QR generator questions

Does this Wi-Fi QR code generator upload my password?

No. The QR encoder in qr.js runs locally inside your browser tab with zero network requests. You can turn off Wi-Fi after loading the page and generate as many codes as you want.

Will iPhone and Android cameras scan the QR code?

Yes. iOS Camera and modern Android cameras natively support the WIFI: QR standard. Need to share a saved password directly from an iPhone to Android without printing? Follow our iPhone to Android Wi-Fi sharing guide.

Does the QR code expire or require a subscription?

No. It is a static QR code with no tracking redirect and no account. You only need to print a new sign if you change your Wi-Fi password.

What extra features are in the Wi-Fi QR iPhone app?

Wi-Fi QR on iPhone and iPad adds 10 printable sign styles (3 free and 7 in one-time Pro), exact 4 x 6 / 5 x 7 / A4 / US Letter sizing, 8 cut-out cards on one sheet, camera scanning for router stickers, and local saved networks for hosts managing multiple properties.
