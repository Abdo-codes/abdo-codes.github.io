WELCOME TO DRIVE TOWN

The road is your classroom.

Tap Drive for a steady pace, Slow for 10 km/h (30 km/h on the merge roads), and Stop for a full stop. Hold Drive to accelerate manually. Use the left and right indicator buttons, then use the button next to Drive. It changes name by lesson: Park when stopped beside the bay, Merge, Overtake or Back to lane. Steering follows the blue route while you decide when it is safe to go.

Guided mode offers coach prompts. Independent mode varies traffic timing and removes safety prompts until you choose Show a hint. Lessons opens all 24 named topics. Every mission ends with a rule check; a wrong answer leads to a changed situation. Independent passes require no hints or wrong answers on that attempt.

Practice in the header opens the Mock exam, the main way to practise: 50 questions in 30 minutes, a pass at 44, with pictures and an overview. Hazard test opens a separate practice of 25 short situations. Camera cycles through Following, Look right and Overview. In the merging, siren and street lessons it cycles Following and Look towards the other side. Look right shows the road beside and behind you before a turn. Recorded decisions use their own fitted view.

Feedback shows the recorded decision and actual speed. Pause or seek the replay, or jump to the decision frame. Progress is saved in this browser.

The seven chapters cover 24 selected Dutch rule topics. All missions end with a short rule check. It is a learning prototype, with original scenarios and links to the RVV 1990.

Drive Town is an independent practice project by Abdelrahman Eita. It is not made by or linked to CBR, and a practice result does not predict your exam result.
