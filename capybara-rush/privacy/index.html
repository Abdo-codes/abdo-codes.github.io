Capybara Rush / Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Overview

Capybara Rush does not collect personal information, track users, or show ads.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game stores progress locally on your device. This includes:

  • Coins
  • Best score
  • Selected capybara
  • Unlocked skins
  • Tutorial state
  • Sound and haptic settings

This data stays on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Game Center

If Game Center is enabled on your device, Apple may process leaderboard data according to Apple's own Game Center and privacy terms.

No Tracking or Advertising

Capybara Rush includes no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs.

No Accounts

Capybara Rush does not require user accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no personal profile.

Contact
