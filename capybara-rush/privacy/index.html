Capybara Rush / Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Overview

Capybara Rush has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It collects basic usage statistics, which do not include your name or contact details, so the game can be improved.

Usage Statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played. It sends:

  • Gameplay and settings events, such as a round started, finished, paused or left; the level played; scores, accuracy and combos in rounded ranges; tutorial progress; skins unlocked or selected; daily challenge progress; Results and Game Center button taps; whether the game asked you to rate it; whether a share was sent or cancelled, and the kind of app it went to (such as Messages, Mail, AirDrop, copy, or a social app); and sound, music, voice or haptics switched on or off.
  • App opens, sessions and time spent in the game, which Firebase records automatically, and whether your copy came from the App Store or TestFlight.
  • A few summary values, such as how many rounds you have played and whether you are signed in to Game Center.
  • A random app-instance ID that Firebase creates on install. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple Account or Game Center profile, and it resets if you delete the app.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, iOS version, app version and language, and an approximate location (such as city, region and country) that Google derives from your IP address. Google Analytics does not store the IP address itself.

The game does not send your name, email address, contacts, photos, Game Center player ID or the text you share. It does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the identifier for vendor (IDFV), does not use the data for advertising, and turns off Google's advertising signals. The data is used only for analytics.

Google Analytics keeps event-level data for up to 14 months. Aggregated reports, which contain no ID, may be kept longer, and the developer may keep a copy of the analytics data to study how the game is played. Google processes the data under its own terms: policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your progress stays on your device, such as coins, best scores, selected level and capybara, unlocked skins, rounds played, daily challenge scores, streak and rewards, tutorial state, and sound, music, voice and haptics settings. The game also keeps a few small records, such as when it last asked you to rate it, whether a round was cut short, and a short list of recent usage events. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, Apple processes your leaderboard scores under Apple's own Game Center and privacy terms.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the daily challenge reminder is scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent anywhere.

Sharing

When you share a result, the system share sheet sends only what you choose to send. The game never sends the shared text itself. It only records, as a usage statistic, that you shared and the kind of app you picked.

No Accounts

Capybara Rush does not require user accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no personal profile.

Questions and Your Data

Because the game has no accounts, the developer cannot connect usage statistics to you. Deleting the app resets the app-instance ID, so new statistics are not linked to the old ones. For privacy questions, contact abdulrhman.eaita@gmail.com.
