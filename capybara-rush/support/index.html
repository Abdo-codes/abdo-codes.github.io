FAQ

How do I play?

Read the prompt and swipe in the requested direction. Some prompts are fake or opposite prompts, so follow the instruction rather than only the arrow.

How long is a round?

The main mode uses quick 30-second rounds.

How do I unlock skins?

Earn coins by playing rounds, then spend coins in the collection screen to unlock new capybara styles.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Capybara Rush works offline. Game Center leaderboards require Apple's Game Center services.

Does the game have ads or tracking?

No. Capybara Rush has no ads, no account requirement, and no tracking SDKs.
