Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add an expense?

Tap the + button on the History tab. Select a category, enter the amount, and optionally add your odometer reading and notes. Tap Save.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to any server. No account or sign-in required.

What is the Registration Chain?

It shows everything needed to renew your vehicle registration (istimaara) in Saudi Arabia — insurance validity, inspection (fahs) status, and traffic fines — in one view.

How does fuel efficiency work?

Log at least two full tank refuels with odometer readings. Dawasah calculates your km/L automatically from the distance traveled between fill-ups.

How do I switch between Arabic and English?

Go to Settings > Language and tap Arabic or English. The entire app — numbers, dates, and currency — adapts instantly.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the Dawasah app from your iPhone. All data is stored locally and will be permanently removed.
