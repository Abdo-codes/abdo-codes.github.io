Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Your Data Stays on Your Device

Dawasah stores all your car expense data locally on your iPhone. Your expenses, vehicle information, odometer readings, and settings are never sent to any external server.

No Account Required

Dawasah does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the app.

What We Collect

We collect anonymous usage analytics through Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and improve the experience. This includes:

  • App launch events and screen views
  • Feature usage (which categories are most used, whether analytics are viewed)
  • Crash reports and performance data

This data is anonymous and cannot be used to identify you personally.

What We Don't Collect

  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • Your expense amounts or financial data
  • Your vehicle details (make, model, plate number)
  • Your location
  • Photos or receipts

Third-Party Services

Dawasah uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — anonymous usage analytics
  • Apple StoreKit — for in-app purchases (Pro subscription)

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

Data Deletion

To delete all your data, simply delete the Dawasah app from your iPhone. All data is stored locally and will be permanently removed when the app is uninstalled.
