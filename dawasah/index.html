Questions

What is Dawasah?

Dawasah is a free car expense tracker for iPhone designed for Saudi drivers. It tracks fuel, maintenance, insurance, registration, and inspection — with smart insights and a registration chain tracker.

Is Dawasah free?

Yes. Core features are completely free. Pro features like unlimited cars, reminders, and advanced reports are available through an optional subscription.

What is the Registration Chain?

It shows everything needed to renew your vehicle registration — insurance, inspection, and fines status — in one view. You always know exactly what to resolve before visiting the traffic department.

Does Dawasah support Arabic?

Fully. Dawasah is Arabic-first with complete English support. Numbers, dates, and currency adapt to your language. Switch between Arabic and English at any time.

Does it calculate fuel consumption?

Yes. After two full tank refuels with odometer readings, Dawasah calculates your fuel efficiency in km/L automatically.

What expenses can I track?

10 categories: Fuel (with liters and price per liter), Oil, Maintenance, Insurance, Registration, Fines, Inspection, Car Wash, Tires, and Other.
