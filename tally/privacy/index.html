Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Overview

Tally has no ads and no accounts, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. From version 1.3.3, the app sends usage analytics and crash reports to Google Firebase. “Analytics and Crash Reports” below tells you what they contain. Version 1.3.2 sends neither. Since version 1.3.2, the app has no in-app purchases.

Data on Your Devices and in iCloud

Tally stores your preferences, team names, and game settings on the device. Starting with version 1.6.0, Tally can sync saved Game Night player names and finished games, including dates, scores, and results, using your private iCloud storage. This lets you see the same history on an iPhone and Apple TV signed in to the same iCloud account. Without iCloud, Tally keeps this data locally.

We do not have access to your private iCloud data. You can remove a saved player or reset your Game Night history from the leaderboard. Firebase does not receive your team names or player names.

Analytics and Crash Reports

Version 1.3.2 sends no usage analytics or crash reports. Versions before 1.3.2 also included Firebase. From version 1.3.3, the app uses two Google Firebase services. Google processes this data for us, and it can process the data in the United States and other countries.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, it sends:

  • The stack trace and the state of the app and device at that time.
  • The device model, the operating system version, and the app version.
  • Random IDs for this installation of the app.

We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs. Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days.

Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase). The app sends:

  • The screens that you open and your progress through the first-run pages.
  • Game events, such as the sport, game start and end, score changes, undo, timer use, rounds, and the game options that you select.
  • The fact that a team name changed, and which team. The event does not include the name.
  • The app version that you first installed, and whether the app came from the App Store or TestFlight.
  • Automatic events from Google’s software, such as first open, app sessions, and app updates.
  • The device model, the operating system version, and the language.
  • An approximate location, such as your country or city, that Google gets from your IP address.
  • Random IDs: an app instance ID, a Firebase installation ID, and Apple’s identifier for vendors (IDFV).

We use analytics to learn which parts of the app people use and to improve them.

What the app never sends to Firebase. Team names and player names.

Your choices. The app has no setting that turns these reports off. To stop them, delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local installation IDs from that device. Reports that the app already sent stay with Google. The app has no account, so we cannot connect these reports to your name. You can email us with a question or a request about your data.

No Accounts

Tally does not require or support user accounts. You can play without signing up. iCloud sync uses the Apple Account already signed in on your device.

No Third-Party Advertising

Tally contains no advertisements, and we do not share app data with advertising networks.

No Cross-App Tracking

Tally does not ask for permission to track you. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Past Purchases

Tally is free. If you bought Tally Pro before version 1.3.2, Apple handled that payment through the App Store. The Restore Purchases row in Settings asks Apple for your purchase history. We never see your payment details.

This Website

This policy is about the Tally app. The yoloabdo.com website uses Google Analytics to count page visits. That is separate from the analytics in the app.

Children's Privacy

Tally does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
