Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Overview

Tally collects no data. The app has no analytics, no ads, no accounts, and no tracking. Since version 1.3.2, the app also has no in-app purchases.

Data Stored on Your Device

All your preferences, team names, and game settings are stored locally on your Apple TV. This data never leaves your device. We do not have access to it.

Analytics

Tally does not collect usage analytics. Earlier versions included Firebase Analytics. We removed it, and the current app sends no usage data to us or to any third party.

No Accounts

Tally does not require or support user accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no personal information collected.

No Third-Party Advertising

Tally contains no advertisements and does not share any data with advertising networks.

No Tracking

Tally does not use any tracking technologies. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Past Purchases

Tally is free. If you bought Tally Pro before version 1.3.2, Apple handled that payment through the App Store. The Restore Purchases row in Settings asks Apple for your purchase history. We never see your payment details.

This Website

This policy is about the Tally app. The yoloabdo.com website uses Google Analytics to count page visits. That does not apply to the app.

Children's Privacy

Tally does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at abdulrhman.eaita@gmail.com.
