Why Tally?

Built exclusively for Apple TV.

Most scoreboard apps are designed for phones and tablets. Tally is different — it's built from the ground up for tvOS, optimized for the 10-foot viewing experience where you're sitting on your couch, not holding a device.

Scores are displayed in giant 220-pixel typography that's readable from across any living room. The Siri Remote is all you need — swipe left or right to pick a team, press Select to score. No second screen, no phone pairing, no account required.

Every sport has its own scoring engine with real rules: basketball tracks quarters with a countdown timer and possession arrow, tennis handles deuce, advantage, and tiebreaks automatically, volleyball uses rally-point scoring with a final set to 15. Undo any action up to 20 steps back.

Tally follows your Apple TV's appearance setting — dark mode for movie nights, light mode for daytime. The dark theme is OLED-friendly with pixel drift protection to prevent burn-in on your Apple TV 4K's display.

Free to try. Upgrade to Pro for all 7 sports, party mode, and more.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What sports does Tally support?
Tally supports 7 sports with full scoring rules: Basketball (4 quarters), Soccer (2 halves), Tennis (best of 3 sets), Ping Pong (best of 5 games), Volleyball (best of 3 sets), Party Game (up to 8 players with custom names, colors, and round timer), and a Generic mode for custom scoring.
Does it work with my Apple TV?
Tally requires tvOS 17 or later and works on both Apple TV 4K and Apple TV HD.
Can I customize team names?
Yes! You can set custom team names before each game in the setup screen.
Does it support VoiceOver?
Absolutely. Tally has full VoiceOver support and respects the Reduce Motion accessibility setting.
Is there a subscription?
Tally is free to try with 2 sports. Upgrade to Tally Pro to unlock all 7 sports, party game mode, player colors, and round timer. Choose weekly, annual, or lifetime — with a free trial.
