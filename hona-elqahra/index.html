Live Activities on Lock Screen & Dynamic Island

When audio begins, Hona Elqahra starts a local-only Live Activity that keeps your walnut radio dial visible on the Lock Screen, StandBy, and Dynamic Island, then clears automatically when you pause or stop.

  • Live amber phase lamp: ON AIR, TUNING, or BUFFERING
  • Round play/pause key and full 6-preset rail (01-06) on the Lock Screen and expanded Dynamic Island

هنا القاهرة

"Hona Elqahra" means "This is Cairo." They were the first words spoken on Egyptian Radio on 31 May 1934, and Egyptian stations still open with them.

Quran Radio from Cairo has broadcast continuously since 25 March 1964. For households across Egypt and the Arab world, it is the familiar sound of the morning. That is why it sits on preset 01 of our dial.

Design Story

We wanted a radio, not a playlist.

Most radio apps are a flat list of streams with a play button. We wanted the presence of a real radio on a shelf: a brass speaker grille, a lit green dial, and a knob you turn. These are the studies from our design files.

01 · A pocket receiver

It started with a different name.

The first sketch was called Aurel, a world receiver for internet radio. It had a brass speaker grille, a dark glass dial, two metal knobs, and a row of six preset keys.

The station was a placeholder. The goal was the object: a radio you want to pick up and touch.

02 · Four radios on the table

We drew four radios before we chose one.

Each concept asked a different question. How much walnut, how much brushed brass? Should the radio fill the screen, or sit inside a room?

03 · Five passes

Then we refined the same radio five times.

We did not restart from scratch. Each pass solved one layout or material detail:

  1. Materials and light: walnut cabinet, champagne brass, and warm dial glow.
  2. Station hierarchy: the broadcasting station name comes first.
  3. Tactile controls: knobs with physical depth and a pressed-in state.
  4. Integrated navigation: every control lives inside the radio face.
  5. Final polish: clear key engravings and a legible tuning scale.
04 · Tuned to Cairo

Then it became هنا القاهرة.

When we tuned the receiver to Quran stations, the whole product clicked. Arabic came first, Quran Radio Cairo became preset 01, and the name took the opening words of Egyptian Radio.

One large tuning knob replaced the dual knobs. Then we studied five ways to keep six stations within thumb reach without cluttering the panel:

The shipped radio has one large knob with six engraved preset marks. Below it, six silver push keys open Stations, Share, Favorites, Sleep Timer (15-90 minutes), AirPlay, and About.

FAQ

Questions

What is Hona Elqahra?

Hona Elqahra is a free Quran radio app for iPhone with 178 live stations inside a walnut and champagne-brass receiver. Tap the knob to play or pause, and turn it across six saved presets starting with Cairo Quran Radio.

How do the iPhone Home Screen widgets work?

Hona Elqahra includes two Preset radio Home Screen widgets. The small widget plays or pauses one pinned or default station, and the wide medium widget shows the active display plus all six preset buttons (01 to 06) synced with your in-app dial.

What does the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island Live Activity show?

When playback starts, the Live Activity appears automatically on your Lock Screen, StandBy display, and Dynamic Island. It displays ON AIR, TUNING, or BUFFERING status, the current station, a round play/pause button, and a six-preset bar so you can switch stations without opening the app. It clears when playback pauses or stops.

Can Siri play Quran radio in Hona Elqahra?

Yes. Say "Play a station on Hona Elqahra" or "Play <station> on Hona Elqahra" to start listening, or "Pause Hona Elqahra" to stop. The Shortcuts app also exposes Play radio station and Pause radio actions.

Which stations and reciters are included?

The bundled catalog includes 178 live stations from MP3Quran plus Cairo Quran Radio: 2 national broadcasts (Cairo and Saudi), 137 reciter and Surah Al-Baqarah streams, 4 tafsir streams, 3 adhkar and ruqyah streams, 10 Islamic programs, and 22 Quran translations across 18 languages. Browse the full 178-station directory.

Can I search by recitation style (riwayah) like Warsh or Mujawwad?

Yes. Station search supports Arabic without diacritics, English phonetic aliases (such as Hosary, Menshawy, Abdelbaset, and Afasy), and 10 recitation styles: Mujawwad, Warsh, Qalun, Khalaf, As-Susi, Ad-Duri, Ad-Duri from Abu Amr, Ibn Dhakwan, Al-Asbahani, and Al-Bazzi.

Is Hona Elqahra free? Are there any ads or subscriptions?

Hona Elqahra is 100% free on the App Store for iPhone with iOS 18 or later. It contains zero ads, zero subscriptions, and zero in-app purchases.

Does Hona Elqahra work without internet?

The 178-station directory is stored on your iPhone so browsing works offline, but all station audio streams live over the internet and may use mobile data. Broadcast availability depends on each broadcaster.

Do I need an account? What data does Hona Elqahra collect?

No account is required. Favorites and six dial presets stay on your iPhone. Usage analytics and Crashlytics are off by default, strictly optional in About, and never log station names, searches, or listening history. Read the privacy policy.

Can I listen to Quran radio on Apple TV?

Hona Elqahra is built strictly for iPhone. For Apple TV (tvOS 17+), Sajdah by the same developer brings Quran radio and live Makkah TV alongside 200+ reciters and prayer times.

What does Hona Elqahra mean, and how do I get support?

Hona Elqahra means "This is Cairo", the opening words spoken on Egyptian Radio on 31 May 1934. For support or broadcaster requests, email abdulrhman.eaita@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version, or visit the support page.
