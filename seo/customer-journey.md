# 5-Stage Customer Journey & AI Search Map: yoloabdo.com

Last updated: 2026-11-22
Scope: Top 4 organic & AI-search growth pillars (`Wi-Fi QR`, `Sajdah`, `Nightcap Awake`, `SwingCoach`) plus `Drive Town` and `Tateemi`.

## Stage 1: Unaware
**User mindset:** Experiencing everyday friction (guests asking for the Wi-Fi password, Mac falling asleep mid-build, losing count during kettlebell swings, forgetting which vaccine dose is due) without searching for a software tool yet.

**Real prompts users type into Google & AI chatbots:**
- "How do I stop my MacBook from sleeping while Xcode or Claude Code runs?"
- "What should I put in an Airbnb welcome binder so guests stop messaging me at check-in?"
- "How do I split 100 kettlebell swings a day without wrecking my lower back?"
- "What vaccines does a 2-month-old baby get in Saudi Arabia in 2026?"
- "How hard is the Dutch CBR car theory exam in English?"

**Content strategy:** Practical, zero-fluff guides that solve the problem directly in the first 40 words and link naturally in the upper 40% to the matching app or free browser tool (already live for 100 swings, Saudi schedule, iPhone-to-Android Wi-Fi, Xcode DerivedData, Git worktrees, and Drive Town guide).

## Stage 2: Problem-Aware
**User mindset:** Knows the solution category (a printable Wi-Fi QR sign, an Apple TV Quran app, an app-based Mac keep-awake utility, a camera rep counter) and looks for a fast free option or workflow.

**Real prompts users type into Google & AI chatbots:**
- "Free Wi-Fi QR code generator printable without creating an account"
- "Can I share my Wi-Fi password from iPhone to Android without typing it?"
- "How do I play Quran and Athan on Apple TV without AirPlay?"
- "Is there a way to keep a Mac awake only while one app is open?"
- "Free mock exam for the new 50-question Dutch CBR theory exam in English"

**Content strategy:** Zero-signup free tools (`/wifi-qr/generator/`, `/drive-town/`) and task guides with real screenshots/outputs so cold search visitors get value in under 30 seconds before seeing an App Store CTA.

## Stage 3: Solution-Aware
**User mindset:** Actively evaluating options and comparing categories (Canva/Etsy vs a dedicated Wi-Fi QR app; Amphetamine vs KeepingYouAwake vs `caffeinate`; Muslim Pro vs Sajdah on Apple TV; OmniSwing/BELLr vs camera-based SwingCoach).

**Real prompts users type into Google & AI chatbots:**
- "Should I buy an Etsy WiFi sign template or use a Wi-Fi QR app for my Airbnb?"
- "What is the best lightweight open-source alternative to Amphetamine on macOS?"
- "Best Quran app for Apple TV with prayer times and Athan compared"
- "Camera kettlebell swing counter app vs Apple Watch rep counter"
- "Best free score keeper app for iPhone and Apple TV with no ads"

**Content strategy:** Honest `/vs/` and comparison pages (`/wifi-qr/vs-canva-etsy/`, `/nightcap-awake/vs-amphetamine/`, `/blog/quran-apps-apple-tv-compared/`) with self-contained competitor blocks, sourced pricing math, and explicit "Where the competitor wins" sections.

## Stage 4: Product-Aware
**User mindset:** Has heard of one of Abdelrahman's apps (from Google, ChatGPT/Perplexity, Reddit, or an in-app SiblingApps list) and checks privacy, pricing, offline support, and platform compatibility before installing.

**Real prompts users type into Google & AI chatbots:**
- "Does Wi-Fi QR upload my Wi-Fi password to a server?"
- "Is Sajdah free on Apple TV or does it require a subscription?"
- "Does Nightcap Awake work with Homebrew and does it keep my screen on?"
- "Does SwingCoach record or upload video from my iPhone camera?"
- "Does Tally work on iPhone as well as Apple TV?"

**Content strategy:** Consistent, plain-text pricing/privacy/platform tables across landing pages, `FAQPage` + `SoftwareApplication` JSON-LD, and `llms.txt` / `llms-full.txt` backed by `seo/kb/brand-ssot.md` so AI answers quote exact, up-to-date answers.

## Stage 5: Most Aware
**User mindset:** Ready to download, install via Homebrew, print a sign, or check what changed in the latest release.

**Real prompts users type into Google & AI chatbots:**
- "Download Sajdah Apple TV App Store"
- "Nightcap Awake brew install cask command"
- "Wi-Fi QR 1.8 cut-out cards"
- "Tally 1.6 iPhone iCloud sync"

**Content strategy:** Fast App Store / Homebrew CTAs above the fold, release-note posts linked bidirectionally from every app page's `#blog` section, and clean `apple-itunes-app` smart banners.
