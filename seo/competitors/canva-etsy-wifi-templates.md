Where Canva and Etsy win (keep these honest on the page):
- Full custom visual branding (custom illustrations, boutique hotel typography, matching house-rules/checkout sign suites).
- Physical laser-engraved wood or acrylic signs shipped ready to hang (Etsy physical sellers).
- Cross-platform desktop collaboration for hospitality teams that already pay for Canva Pro across menus and guidebooks.
- One-off guests standing right next to you on iOS 18+ (Apple's built-in Passwords app requires zero downloads).
