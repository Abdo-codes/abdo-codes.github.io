Quran app for Apple TV: Sajdah 1.7.4 previews Premium

Sajdah 1.7.4 shows a preview of the Premium feature you pick, including scenic backgrounds. It also improves Quran Radio, prayer times and Apple TV remote navigation.

Abdelrahman Eita · 19 November 2026 · 3 min read

Sajdah is a Quran and Athan app for Apple TV. Version 1.7.4 is on the App Store now. This update follows Sajdah 1.7.3. Here is what changed.

In short

  • Premium preview: the offer shows the feature you picked.
  • Scenic backgrounds: Nature is free, and six scenes are Premium.
  • Quran Radio: retry a station or switch to another one.
  • Prayer times: clear labels and a fix for the end of daylight saving time.
  • Remote navigation and more.

Preview the Premium feature you pick

When you pick a locked feature, the Premium offer now shows a preview of that feature.

  • Choose a Quran font, and the offer shows that font.
  • Select a scenic background, and the offer shows that scene.
  • Use speed or repeat during your first listen, and the offer matches the tool you tried.

Listening to the Quran is always free. Press Back or Menu on the remote to close an offer.

The one-time Lifetime purchase is in Settings, under Lifetime Premium. Feature offers show the yearly and monthly plans.

Scenic backgrounds

Nature is the free video background. Premium adds six scenes: Deer Creek Falls, Joshua Tree Sunset, Redwood Fog, Panamint Mountains, Frenchman Bay and Sand Ripples. Four work offline. Two are downloads.

To try one:

  1. Open Settings and go to Video Background.
  2. Select a scene with a Premium lock.
  3. Look at the preview in the offer.
  4. If you unlock Premium, Sajdah applies the scene.

Quran Radio: retry or switch

If a station stops, the playback bar now shows Retry. It also offers another available station.

Prayer times

  • If Sajdah cannot find your location, it labels the Makkah times as a reference.
  • With Premium, choose a city for local prayer times and the Athan.
  • Prayer times now read correctly in the extra hour when daylight saving time ends.

Remote navigation

The Back button on the Apple TV remote returns focus to the item you left, after playback. Favorite reciters have a mark on their cards. VoiceOver reads more controls in Settings.

Also in this update

  • Khatmah on Today: track all 114 surahs, choose a reciter, and set an optional 30-day pace.
  • Find a surah: search by Arabic name, transliteration, English meaning or number.
  • A clearer first ayah: the player shows the opening Basmala apart from the first ayah.

Questions

How do I preview a Premium feature in Sajdah? Open Settings, then select a scenic background that has a lock. Sajdah opens an offer with a preview of that scene.

Are the scenic backgrounds free? Nature is free. The six scenic backgrounds are part of Sajdah Premium.

Can I listen to Quran radio on Apple TV with Sajdah? Yes. Quran Radio is part of Sajdah Premium. If a station stops, retry it or switch to another station.

Does Sajdah show prayer times on Apple TV? Yes. Today shows your next prayer. With Premium, choose a city for local prayer times and the Athan.

Get the update

Sajdah 1.7.4 is free to download on the App Store for Apple TV. Update or download it on the App Store. If something does not work as you expect, write to me through Sajdah support.
