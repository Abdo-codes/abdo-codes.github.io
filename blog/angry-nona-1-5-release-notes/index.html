Angry Nona 1.5: Topsy-Turvy Week brings six new nights

Nights 20 to 25 are here, so the campaign now has 25 nights. You can share your Daily Night as a grid of squares, and you can hear the game even when your iPhone is on silent.

Abdelrahman Eita · 10 November 2026 · 3 min read

Angry Nona: Night Shift is a cozy iPhone game about one long night in a nursery. You keep six toddlers asleep until dawn. Version 1.5 adds Topsy-Turvy Week: six new nights, and each night turns one rule upside down.

In short

  • Nights 20 to 25: the campaign now has 25 nights.
  • Share your Daily Night as a grid of squares, with new Topsy-Turvy recipes.
  • Sound plays on silent, and your own music keeps playing.
  • Privacy, Support and Send Feedback links in Settings.
  • Full Shift owners get the new nights at no cost.

Topsy-Turvy Week: Nights 20 to 25

Each new night brings one new rule. The game tells you the rule in one short line before the trouble starts.

  • Night 20, Bedtime Rush. All six toddlers start awake. "Everyone's up. Tuck them in." You win when all six sleep at the same time.
  • Night 21, Three Bells. Trouble comes in three waves. A bell rings before each wave, and a short quiet time follows it. Use the quiet for your chores.
  • Night 22, Crossed Wires. Yeetie's ducks land inside Pea's glow. Tidy them, but do it without the coffee cart's fast feet.
  • Night 23, Mom Checks In. Mom's hall light comes on three times. While it is on, every tap makes noise. "Hall light on: hands off."
  • Night 24, Blackout. The power cuts in the middle of the night. The thought bubbles show "?" until you relight the lamp.
  • Night 25, Topsy-Turvy. The finale. The waves, the ducks, Mom's checks and the blackout all come in one night, one rule at a time.

If you last played at launch, you also missed The Sleepover from version 1.2: Nights 14 to 19, diaper duty, and four new chores (night-light, laundry, music box and pacifier steamer).

Share your Daily Night

The Daily Night is one night that every player gets on the same day. When you finish it, the result card now shows your night as a grid of squares. Each column is a toddler, with a crown for Nona. Each row is a time, from 9 PM to 5 AM. A square shows if that toddler slept, stirred or cried.

Tap Share my night to send the grid to a friend, or tap the copy button to paste it anywhere.

The Daily Night also has new Topsy-Turvy recipes. Some days, a Topsy-Turvy rule turns up in the Daily Night.

Sound plays on silent

Before 1.5, the game stayed quiet when your iPhone was on silent. Now it plays its sound on silent too. If you play your own music, it keeps playing. To mute the game, use the Music and Sounds switches in Settings.

Privacy, Support and Send Feedback

Settings now has links to the privacy policy, to support, and to Send Feedback. If a night feels too hard or too easy, please tell me there.

What is free

Nights 1 to 4 are still free. If you own Full Shift, the six new nights are yours at no cost. Full Shift is one purchase, not a subscription.

Get the update

Angry Nona: Night Shift 1.5 is on the App Store now. It needs an iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Update or download it on the App Store, and try to get through Night 25. New to the game? Read the launch post first. Sweet dreams.
