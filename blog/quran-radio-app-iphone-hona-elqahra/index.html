Quran radio app for iPhone: Hona Elqahra is out

I opened a Quran radio app and it was bad. It played the stations, but it did not feel calm, and it did not feel like a radio. So I built my own. Hona Elqahra (هنا القاهرة) is on the App Store today, and it is free.

Abdelrahman Eita · 18 November 2026 · 4 min read

Today Apple approved Hona Elqahra: Quran Radio, version 1.0. It is a Quran radio app for iPhone. You tap one large knob to play, and you turn it to change the station. Quran Radio Cairo is the first station on the dial. More than 170 live Quran radio stations are one tap away.

In short

  • Why I built it: a bad radio app.
  • A radio, not a playlist: one knob and six stations.
  • The stations: Cairo Quran Radio, Saudi Quran Radio and famous reciters.
  • The feeling: haptic clicks, radio hiss and a grille you can touch.
  • Quran radio on Apple TV: it is also in Sajdah.
  • No account, and the app never starts playing on its own.

It started with a bad radio app

One day I opened a Quran radio app on my iPhone, and it was a bad experience. It played the streams, but it did not feel calm, and it did not feel like a radio.

Listening to the Quran radio should be simple. You turn it on, and it plays. Many of us grew up with a real radio at home, tuned to Quran Radio from Cairo. I wanted my phone to give me that same feeling. So I closed that app and started to design my own.

A radio, not a playlist

Most radio apps show a long list of streams and a play button. I wanted a real radio on the screen: a speaker grille, a lit dial and a knob that you turn. Before the name, I drew four different radios. I picked one and refined it five times.

The name came last. هنا القاهرة (“This is Cairo”) were the first words on Egyptian Radio in 1934, and Egyptian stations still open with them. It felt right for a radio that starts with Cairo.

The Quran radio stations

Six stations sit on the knob when you first open the app. You can change any of them:

  1. Quran Radio Cairo (إذاعة القرآن الكريم من القاهرة), the Egyptian Quran radio, on air since 1964.
  2. Holy Quran Radio from Saudi Arabia (إذاعة القرآن الكريم من السعودية).
  3. Mishary Alafasy radio.
  4. Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary radio.
  5. Abdulbasit Abdulsamad radio.
  6. Mohamed Siddiq Al-Minshawi radio.

Open the station list to find more than 170 live stations: reciters, tafsir, adhkar and Quran translation stations. Search in Arabic or English, filter by category, and save your favorites. You can put any station on any of the six knob positions.

The station list comes from the MP3Quran radio directory, plus Quran Radio Cairo. Hona Elqahra is an independent player. The audio belongs to the broadcasters.

A radio you can feel

A real radio answers your hand, so I spent a lot of time on touch and sound:

  • Station clicks. You feel a short haptic tick at each station, and a firmer stop at 01 and 06.
  • A knob that presses in. It sinks when you touch it and stays slightly seated while it plays.
  • Radio hiss. A soft hiss plays while you tune between stations.
  • A grille you can touch. Rub the speaker grille to feel soft ribs and hear a quiet brush sound.
  • Real volume keys. Volume uses the iPhone buttons, like a radio's own dial.

It also does what a listening app must do: background audio, AirPlay, lock-screen controls and a sleep timer. The app works in Arabic and English.

Quran radio on Apple TV: Sajdah

Hona Elqahra is for your iPhone. If you want Quran radio on the big screen, my Apple TV app Sajdah has it too. Sajdah has Quran radio stations and live Islamic TV, next to 200+ reciters, prayer times and the Athan. Quran radio and live TV are part of Sajdah Premium.

So you can listen on your phone during the day, and on the TV in the living room at night. Read more in the comparison of Quran apps for Apple TV.

No account, no surprises

  • No account. Favorites and presets stay on your iPhone.
  • Optional analytics. Usage analytics and crash reports stay off until you choose them, and you can change them in About.
  • No surprise audio. When you open the app again, it never starts playing on its own.

Listening needs internet and may use mobile data. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Questions

How can I listen to Cairo Quran Radio on iPhone? Install Hona Elqahra and tap the large knob. Quran Radio Cairo is preset 01, so it plays first.

Is there a free Quran radio app for iPhone? Yes. Hona Elqahra is free to download for iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Can I listen to Quran radio on Apple TV? Yes, with Sajdah. Quran radio is part of Sajdah Premium.
