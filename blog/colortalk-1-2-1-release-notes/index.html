ColorTalk 1.2.1: Four Open Picture Books, Living Dinosaurs & Four-Book Printing

Every book now opens as a real two-page picture book, all 20 dinosaurs come alive in their own prehistoric habitats with storybook adventures, and parents can print pages from all four books.

Abdelrahman Eita · 24 November 2026 · 4 min read

ColorTalk is a calm, offline coloring app on iPad for children ages 2 to 5. Version 1.2.1 has been live on the App Store since 24 November 2026, building directly on ColorTalk 1.2. The bookshelf look on the Home screen now extends all the way inside every book.

In short

  • Four tactile open picture books: ABC, 123, Arabic Letters, and Dinosaurs all flip as two-page spreads with your child's art.
  • 20 living dinosaurs: Dino Valley is replaced by prehistoric habitats, facts on arrival, and 20 gentle storybook adventures.
  • Coloring studio upgrades: Lumo cheers beside the canvas, brighter Glow & Sparkle, undoable clear, and 64 pt buttons.
  • Parents upgrades: AirPrint colored or blank pages from all four books, today's play time, and Lumo voice preview.

Four tactile open picture books

Instead of flat grids inside each book, all four books—ABC (A to Z), 123 (1 to 10), Arabic Letters (28 letters), and Dinosaurs (20 dinosaurs)—now share a stitched, felt-bound two-page picture book spread that matches its cover on the shelf.

  • Your child's art on every page. Completed pages display your child's own colored drawing right on the paper, while unfinished pages show crisp outlines ready to tap.
  • Page turns and quick-jump dots. Flip spreads by touch or tap the page dots along the bottom to jump straight to any spread, with per-book spread memory so you return where you left off.
  • Natural reading direction in every language. Across all 6 app languages (English, British English, Dutch, Arabic, German, and French), ABC and 123 always read left-to-right, Arabic Letters always reads right-to-left, and Dinosaurs follows the app language.

20 living dinosaurs and gentle storybook adventures

Version 1.2.1 retires Dino Valley and brings every finished dinosaur into its own prehistoric habitat. Color any of the 20 dinosaurs, watch your child's drawing come alive with its own walking gait and wildlife call, and explore at a gentle preschool pace:

  • A discovery on arrival. Meet each dinosaur where it lived and spot how it ate, moved, or protected itself—such as finding the club on Ankylosaurus's tail—localized across all 6 interface languages.
  • 20 calm storybook adventures. Tap Play from the open book to start a quiet, interactive adventure and collect felt keepsakes back on the book spread.
  • Quiet-time design. Just like the Arabic Letters book, the Dinosaurs book stays voice-free so kids can explore peacefully before bedtime. Over in the ABC book, finishing the butterfly also morphs it into a life-size monarch.

Coloring studio upgrades

Small details make coloring smoother for toddlers and preschoolers:

  • Lumo cheers beside the canvas. Lumo gives gentle encouragement next to the drawing instead of covering the page.
  • Brighter Glow and Sparkle tools. Both special brushes now stand out clearly even over bright crayon fills.
  • Undoable region clear & rainbow intro. Clearing a colored area can be undone with a single tap, and a gentle swipe-to-color rainbow welcomes kids in, backed by 64 pt tap targets throughout.

Four-book printing and Parents upgrades

Open the Parents area behind the grown-up math gate to see everything added for families:

  • Print from all four books. Send colored keepsakes or blank outlines straight to paper via AirPrint from ABC, 123, Arabic Letters, and Dinosaurs.
  • Today's play time and adventure progress. View progress across all four books, words explored, and how long your child played today.
  • Voice preview and support tools. Preview Lumo's recorded English voice before toggling Spoken Prompts (still off by default), copy the support email in one tap, and browse Artwork & Sound Credits in Settings.

Free, offline, and Kids-safe

ColorTalk 1.2.1 stays 100% free on iPad with no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases, and no external links anywhere in the child flow. All four books and Lumo's voice work completely offline, and the app sends only Kids-safe anonymous usage counts with no crash reports and no ad tracking. Questions

How do the open picture books work in ColorTalk 1.2.1? Every book—ABC, 123, Arabic Letters, and Dinosaurs—now opens as a real two-page picture book spread. Kids turn pages by touch or use quick-jump page dots, and every page displays the child's own colored artwork.

What changed in the Dinosaurs book? ColorTalk 1.2.1 replaces Dino Valley with 20 living dinosaurs inside their own prehistoric habitats. When your child finishes coloring a dinosaur, it comes alive with a walking animation and wildlife call, teaches a short discovery fact, and invites them into a calm storybook adventure with felt keepsakes.

Can parents print coloring pages from every book? Yes. Behind the grown-up math gate in Parents, you can print either your child's colored pages or blank pages on paper via AirPrint from all four books: ABC, 123, Arabic Letters, and Dinosaurs.

Does ColorTalk 1.2.1 stay free and offline with no ads? Yes. ColorTalk 1.2.1 is free on iPad with no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases, and no external links in the child flow. Everything works offline, sending only Kids-safe anonymous usage counts with no crash reports.
