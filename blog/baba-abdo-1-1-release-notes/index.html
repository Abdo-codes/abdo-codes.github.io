Baba Abdo 1.1: Peacock & Brass UI and live language switch

Baba Abdo 1.1 is live on the App Store with a refreshed Peacock & Brass Lantern look, an instant Arabic and English switcher inside the app, and clearer Day 1 kitchen cues.

Abdelrahman Eita · 25 November 2026

Baba Abdo 1.1 is now out on the App Store. After shipping 1.0, this update focuses on readability, visual craft, and making it effortless to switch between Arabic and English without touching iOS system settings.

  • Peacock & Brass Lantern UI
  • Live Arabic and English switcher
  • Clearer Day 1 kitchen & English LTR polish
  • Sticker gallery & sharing polish
  • Questions

Peacock & Brass Lantern UI

Menus, cards, and buttons across Home, Settings, Catalog, and the Shop Stickers album now share the Peacock & Brass Lantern look:

  • Compact two-tier Home mode cards with gold medallions and leading SF Symbol action icons.
  • Gold-headed continuation card on Home and evening-peacock modal scrims across sheets.
  • Tactile button spring press feedback paired with subtle haptics.

Live Arabic and English switcher

You can now flip between Arabic and English live inside the app, persisted across launches:

  • Tap the top-right EN / عربي pill on the Home screen, or use the Language row inside Settings.
  • Switching updates menus, HUD layout direction, chalk menu prices, storefront lettering, and localized sticker views right away.
  • Left-to-right English Settings toggles, Pause column order, and display headings (Lalezar-Regular) are tuned to fit cleanly on small and large iPhones alike.

Clearer Day 1 kitchen & English LTR polish

  • Day 1 kitchen clarity: tighter opening spacing, cleaner tray label spacing in English, and hiding the empty timer bar on the untimed Day 1 teaching order.
  • Condiment contrast: high-contrast Peacock pills behind the Shatta (chili) and Daqqa (garlic vinegar) bottle labels so they stay legible at a glance.
  • Day 1 onboarding & copy clarity: clearer guidance for first-time cooks in both Arabic and English.

Sticker gallery & sharing polish

The Shop Stickers sheet now displays an equal-height 4×2 sticker gallery grid with improved sharing discoverability for WhatsApp, Messages, and receipt sharing.

Questions

Can I switch Baba Abdo between Arabic and English inside the app? Yes. Use the language pill at the top corner of the Home screen or open Settings and tap the language row to switch between Arabic and English immediately.

Is Baba Abdo 1.1 free? Yes. Baba Abdo is free on iPhone with no ads and no in-app purchases. All shop money is earned and spent inside the game.
