A score keeper app for iPhone and Apple TV: Tally 1.6

Tally is a free score keeper app for iPhone and Apple TV. Version 1.6 is out now. It adds iPhone, a new home screen, a chess clock and more game types.

Abdelrahman Eita · 15 November 2026 · 4 min read

If you want a score keeper app for iPhone, Tally 1.6 is for you. Tally was an Apple TV scoreboard. Since version 1.6.0, released on 13 November 2026, it also runs on iPhone. It is free, with no ads, and it keeps score for card games, board games, darts and cornhole.

In short

  • Tally on iPhone, with saved players and games synced through iCloud.
  • A new home screen that shows who leads the house.
  • A chess clock with optional time added after each move.
  • Game types for rummy, hearts, darts, cornhole and more.
  • How to keep score on iPhone, step by step.

Tally is now on iPhone

You can keep score in your hand or on the big screen. Saved players and finished games sync with iCloud, so your iPhone and your Apple TV show the same history. Tally needs iOS 17 or later on iPhone, and tvOS 17 or later on Apple TV.

You can also resume a game within 24 hours. If the Apple TV sleeps or the app closes, open Tally and continue.

A home screen with the house leaderboard

The home screen shows who leads the house before you start. Each saved player has wins, games played and a win rate. A Resume row shows your last game. Three tiles start Game Night, a two-team Scoreboard or the chess clock.

A chess clock app, built in

The chess clock has two clocks, one for each player. Choose 1, 3, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes. You can add 2, 5 or 10 seconds after each move. Use it for chess or for any timed game. It also runs on Apple TV.

A board game score keeper for many games

Game Night supports 2 to 8 players. Pick a game type and Tally sets the target and the score steps for you. You can also make your own game.

  • Card Game 500: first to 500.
  • Word Game: a running total.
  • Rummy 500: a rummy 500 score keeper. First to 500.
  • Hearts: the lowest score wins at 100.
  • Darts 501: a darts 501 scorer. The score counts down to 0.
  • Cornhole: first to 21.

Tally has three ways to score: Running Total, Rounds Won and Lives. The number pad lets you enter any score. Tally tells you when a player reaches the target. You can undo the last 20 moves, and you can set an optional 5 or 10 minute timer.

Tally for Apple TV also has scoreboards for basketball, soccer, tennis, ping pong and volleyball. The Apple TV scoreboard shows giant numbers that the whole room can read.

How to keep score on iPhone

  1. Open Tally and tap Game Night.
  2. Add 2 to 8 players. Pick saved players, or tap Add player.
  3. Choose a game type, for example Rummy 500. Change the target or the timer if you want.
  4. Tap Start game.
  5. Tap a player card to add points. For a big score, open the number pad and enter the amount.
  6. When a player reaches the target, Tally tells you. The game goes into the house leaderboard.

Price and privacy

Tally is free, and every feature is included. It has no ads and no accounts. Version 1.6.0 sends usage analytics and crash reports to Google Firebase. It does not send player names or team names. iCloud sync uses the Apple Account on your device, and I cannot read your private iCloud data. The privacy policy has the details.

Get Tally 1.6

Download Tally on the App Store for iPhone and Apple TV. If a game type is missing or a score is wrong, write to me through Tally support.
