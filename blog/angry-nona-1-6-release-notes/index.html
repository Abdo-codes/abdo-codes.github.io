Angry Nona 1.6: a reason to come back each night

Version 1.6 gives you a reason to come back each night. Play on back-to-back days to earn streak rewards, see how your Daily Night compares with your friends, and get a new text from Mom each day.

Abdelrahman Eita · 11 November 2026 · 3 min read

Angry Nona: Night Shift is an iPhone game about one nanny who keeps six toddlers asleep until 6:30 AM. Version 1.6 is on its way to the App Store and rolls out after App Store review. The theme is simple: a reason to come back each night.

In short

  • Streak rewards for the Daily Night, with three items you cannot buy.
  • Friends today: your Daily Night score next to your Game Center friends.
  • Mom texts a new line each day, and tells you when a reward is one night away.
  • A What's New screen one time after a big update.
  • Arabic Game Center achievements, and a new one for Night 25.
  • Free and paid players get all of this.

Streak rewards for the Daily Night

The Daily Night is one new night each day. It is the same for every player, and the day changes at midnight UTC. Play it on back-to-back days to build a streak. The streak pays you back:

  • Day 2: 3,000 shop points.
  • Day 3: 6,000 shop points.
  • Day 5: 12,000 shop points.
  • Day 7: the Night Owl rug.
  • Day 14: the Dawn Shift outfit.
  • Day 30: the Moon Crown hair style.

The rug, the outfit and the hair style cannot be bought. Only the streak gives them. Each Daily Night also gives a small streak bonus: 500 points for each streak day, up to 5,000.

The streak is gentle. If you miss one day, it does not reset. If you miss a longer gap, it is cut in half, not erased.

Friends today

After your Daily Night, a short list shows your score next to your Game Center friends for that day. The Daily leaderboard now opens on the friends view. This needs Game Center. The game works without it.

Mom texts a new line each day

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, Mom now sends a new line each day. When a streak reward is one night away, she tells you.

A What's New screen, and a way to rate

After a big update, the game now shows a What's New? screen one time. A short screen follows it. There you can rate the game or send feedback. It never interrupts a night.

Arabic Game Center achievements

All 15 Game Center achievements now have Arabic text. There is also a new achievement for Night 25.

What is free

Free and paid players both get all of this. Nights 1 to 4 are free. Full Shift is one purchase that unlocks Nights 5 to 25 and Endless. The game has no ads. Points come from play only, and you cannot buy points.

One limit to know about: the streak, your points and the items you earn stay on your device. They do not sync with iCloud yet.

Get the update

Angry Nona: Night Shift 1.6 is in App Store review now and rolls out after it. Open the game on the App Store to download it or to update when it is ready. Read what came before in the 1.5 release notes. If something does not work, write to support. Sweet dreams.
