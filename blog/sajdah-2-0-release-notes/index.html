Sajdah 2.0 for Apple TV: Ramadan Living Room Mode, Free Prayer Times & Family Hifz

Sajdah 2.0 brings Ramadan 1448 Living Room Mode, free five daily prayer times with the Horizon Prayer Screen, Family Hifz, Playlists, Juz 1–30 jump, Quick Tafsir, Adhkar Auto-Pace, and 60-second daily Radio & Live TV previews.

Abdelrahman Eita · 24 November 2026 · 4 min read

Sajdah is a native Quran, prayer times, and Athan app built for Apple TV with zero ads and no AirPlay required. Following Sajdah 1.7.4, version 2.0 has been submitted to Apple for review and will roll out to Apple TV as soon as Apple approves it.

In short

  • Ramadan 1448 Living Room Mode (Free): live Iftar & Suhoor countdowns, automatic Iftar dua, 30-day Khatmah, Makkah Taraweeh, and Hijri crescent offset.
  • Free prayer times & Horizon Prayer Screen: all five daily prayers and the Horizon Prayer Screen are now free, with 6 styles in total.
  • Family Hifz for all 114 surahs (Free): guided listen-and-repeat memorization from Today.
  • Playlists, Juz 1–30 jump & Quick Tafsir: starter playlists, Juz browsing, and Tafsir Al-Muyassar.
  • 60-second daily Live previews, Adhkar Auto-Pace & unified sky: try Radio & Live TV daily, and keep the Home Screen sky across screens.

Ramadan 1448 Living Room Mode

Sajdah 2.0 prepares the living room TV for Ramadan from the moment you turn it on:

  • Iftar & Suhoor countdowns. Live countdowns appear on Today, the Apple TV Home Screen Top Shelf, and the free Horizon Prayer Screen.
  • Automatic Iftar dua. During the final 10 minutes before Maghrib, the authentic Iftar dua surfaces automatically on screen.
  • Free Hijri date adjustment. Shift the Hijri date by -2 to +2 days in Settings to match your local moon sighting.
  • 1-tap 30-day Ramadan Khatmah & Live Makkah Taraweeh. Start a one-Juz-a-day Khatmah with one click from Today, and find Live Makkah Taraweeh pinned on Ramadan evenings.

Free daily prayer times & Horizon Prayer Screen

Checking when the next prayer starts shouldn't sit behind a paywall. Starting in 2.0, all five daily prayer times for your location and the Horizon Prayer Screen are free for every household, with zero ads.

Sajdah Premium remains available (monthly, yearly, or as a one-time Lifetime unlock in Settings) for the other 5 alternate Prayer Screen styles (Classic, Focus, Timetable, Rosette, and Board — 6 styles in total in the submitted 2.0 build), 13 Athan voices with a dedicated Fajr Athan, Qibla direction on the big screen, 15 mosque calculation methods, and prayer time adjustments. Five additional Prayer Screen styles and a Quick Control Dock are planned for a follow-up update and are not part of this submitted 2.0 build.

Family Hifz for all 114 surahs

Open Family Hifz directly from Today for guided listen-and-repeat memorization across all 114 surahs, built so parents and children can memorize together on the living room TV for free.

Playlists, Juz 1–30 jump & Quick Tafsir

Navigating and listening across the mushaf gets much faster in 2.0:

  • Playlists. Play curated starter sets—such as Friday Al-Kahf, Before Sleep, and Ruqyah—plus any saved playlists for free. Creating, adding to, and editing your own custom playlists unlocks with Sajdah Premium.
  • Juz 1–30 quick jump. Filter surah browsing by any Juz from 1 to 30 for free.
  • Quick Tafsir inside the player. Read concise Tafsir Al-Muyassar (and English explanation on Hafs recordings) right inside the player for free.

60-second daily Live previews, Adhkar Auto-Pace & unified sky

  • 60-second daily Live previews (Free). Browse all Quran Radio stations and live Islamic TV channels (including Makkah) and sample 60 seconds of Radio and Live TV each day for free. Unlimited streaming remains in Sajdah Premium.
  • Hands-free Adhkar Auto-Pace (Free). Morning and Evening Adhkar can advance automatically at a calm reading pace—or stick with Siri Remote tap counting.
  • Unified sky. The sky backdrop from Home now carries consistently into Playlists, Prayer Times, and Settings.

Questions

Are daily prayer times and the Prayer Screen free in Sajdah 2.0? Yes. All five daily prayer times for your location, the Horizon Prayer Screen, and the Hijri date adjustment are free with zero ads. Sajdah Premium unlocks the other 5 alternate Prayer Screen styles (Classic, Focus, Timetable, Rosette, and Board — 6 styles in total in the submitted 2.0 build), 13 Athan voices, Qibla, 15 calculation methods, and prayer time adjustments.

What does Ramadan Living Room Mode include? In Ramadan, Sajdah displays live Iftar and Suhoor countdowns on Today, Top Shelf, and the Horizon Prayer Screen, shows the authentic Iftar dua 10 minutes before Maghrib, starts a 30-day Ramadan Khatmah with one press, pins Live Makkah Taraweeh in the evenings, and lets you adjust the Hijri date by -2 to +2 days for your local moon sighting.

Can I use Playlists without Sajdah Premium? Yes. Playing the curated starter playlists (Friday Al-Kahf, Before Sleep, Ruqyah) and any saved playlists is free. Creating, adding to, and editing custom playlists requires Sajdah Premium.

When will Sajdah 2.0 be available on Apple TV? Sajdah 2.0 has been submitted to Apple for review and will roll out automatically on the App Store for Apple TV once Apple approves the update.

Does Sajdah work offline? No. Sajdah requires an internet connection to stream Quran recitations, Quran Radio, live Islamic TV channels, and prayer data.

Get Sajdah on Apple TV

Search for Sajdah in the App Store on your Apple TV (tvOS 17 or later), or view Sajdah on the App Store. Once Apple approves 2.0, the update will install automatically if automatic updates are enabled. Have a feature request? Write through Sajdah support.
