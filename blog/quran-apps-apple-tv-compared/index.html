The best Quran apps for Apple TV, compared (2026)

I found nine Quran apps that run on Apple TV. Here is what each one confirms for the TV, what it costs, and who it suits. Every row has a source.

Abdelrahman Eita · 17 October 2026 · 6 min read

Disclosure first: I make Sajdah, one of the apps in this list. I tried to be fair. Where another app is stronger, I say so. You can check every claim in the sources.

In short

  • Nine Quran apps have a confirmed Apple TV version. Three are built only for Apple TV: Sajdah, Quran Mushaf TV and the Holy Quran TV!. Six are phone apps with a TV version.
  • Sajdah puts recitation, the Quran text, prayer times with Athan, live TV and Top Shelf in one TV app. It needs the internet.
  • Quran Mushaf TV shows the Mushaf page on your TV, adds translation and tafsir, and works offline.
  • Ameen and Quran Pro say that you can listen to recitations on Apple TV.
  • For Muslim Pro, Quran TV, Quran Radios and Quran+, the store page does not describe the TV app. They are worth a try if you already use them on your phone.

How we compared

I checked every app on 17 October 2026 in the US App Store. An app is in this list only if its App Store page shows an Apple TV entry with a tvOS version under "Compatibility".

This comparison is about the TV only: what you can do from the sofa with the Siri Remote. I looked at listening, following the Quran text, live streams, prayer times on the TV, Top Shelf, ads on the TV and price.

For an app that is built only for Apple TV, everything on its store page is about the TV. For a phone app with a TV version, most of the page is about the iPhone. I count a feature only if the page says it works on Apple TV. Otherwise the table says "Not stated for Apple TV". This does not mean the feature is missing. It means the listing does not say.

I left out store ratings. For a phone app, the rating covers every device, so it does not tell you about the TV app. I did not install and test each app on an Apple TV.

The apps, one by one

Sajdah (built only for Apple TV)

Sajdah is my app. Every screen is built for the Siri Remote. It has 200+ reciters and verse sync for free. With Premium, words light up as they are recited on selected reciters, and you get prayer times with Athan, Qibla, Quran radio and live Islamic TV. Top Shelf shows where you stopped, the verse of the day and the next prayer. The release notes list what is free. The last update was on 8 October 2026.

Weak points: Sajdah needs the internet, because it streams. Prayer times, Athan and live TV need Premium.

Quran Mushaf TV (built only for Apple TV)

Quran Mushaf TV shows the Madani Hafs Mushaf page on the TV, and the developer says scholars reviewed every page. You can read with translation and tafsir, play recitation, repeat a verse and bookmark verses. It works offline, which Sajdah does not. Version 3.9.2, from 14 October 2026, added the IndoPak Mushaf with auto-scroll. It contains ads, and premium plans start at $0.99 a week.

the Holy Quran TV! (built only for Apple TV)

This is a $4.99 app with one reciter, Sheikh Abdelbasit Abdelsamad, and verse-by-verse recitation. Its store text was written for the iPad version. Its last update was on 4 March 2016.

Quran Pro (phone app with a TV version)

Quran Pro's page says that one subscription lets you listen on Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay. If you already pay for Quran Pro on your phone, that is a strong reason to try it on the TV. The page does not describe the TV screens, so I cannot say what else the TV app does.

Ameen (phone app with a TV version)

Ameen is a charity (waqf) project. It is a free download, and the page lists no in-app purchases. Its recitation player supports Apple TV. The page does not say which other features are on the TV.

Muslim Pro, Quran TV, Quran Radios and Quran+

These four are phone apps with a confirmed tvOS version, but their pages do not describe the TV app. Muslim Pro, Quran TV (from the Quran Majeed team) and Quran Radios are free downloads, so you can try them on your TV at no cost. Quran+ costs $7.99. I cannot compare their TV features from the listings.

Which app fits which person

  • You want one TV app for the whole home, with recitation, the Quran text, prayer times, Athan, live Makkah TV and Top Shelf: Sajdah. (I make it, so weigh that.)
  • You read from the Mushaf page, or your internet is weak: Quran Mushaf TV. It works offline. Sajdah does not.
  • You want a free recitation player with no purchases: Ameen.
  • You already pay for Quran Pro on your phone: its subscription covers listening on Apple TV.
  • You already use Muslim Pro, Quran TV or Quran Radios: download the TV version and see if it fits your living room.

Apps I checked but left out

These apps have no tvOS entry on their App Store page, so they are not in the table: Quran Majeed, Quran by Quran.com, Al Quran (Tafsir & by Word), iQuran, Ayah, MP3Quran and Tarteel. The release notes of Quran Audio by Quran.com mention Apple TV support, but its page lists no tvOS version, so I could not confirm it. Al Quran Tv appears in web search results, but its App Store page did not load in the US, UK, Saudi, UAE, Tunisia or France stores.
