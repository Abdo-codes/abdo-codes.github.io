The best Quran apps for Apple TV, compared (2026)

Nine Quran apps have a confirmed Apple TV (tvOS) build. Here is what each one actually supports from the sofa, what it costs, and which fits your living room — with a source for every row.

Abdelrahman Eita · Updated 23 November 2026 · 7 min read

Disclosure first: I make Sajdah, one of the apps in this list. I tried to be fair. Where another app is stronger (for example, offline Mushaf reading), I say so. You can verify every claim in the sources.

In short · Quick picks

  • Nine Quran apps have a confirmed Apple TV version. Three are built only for Apple TV: Sajdah, Quran Mushaf TV and the Holy Quran TV!. Six are phone apps with a TV version.
  • Best all-in-one living-room app (reciters + Prayer Screen + Athan + Live TV, no ads): Sajdah (tvOS 17+, free with optional monthly, yearly or one-time Lifetime Premium). Needs internet.
  • Best for offline Mushaf page reading & tafsir: Quran Mushaf TV (Madani & IndoPak pages, works offline; contains ads).
  • Best free waqf recitation player: Ameen (no in-app purchases listed).
  • Already subscribe on iPhone? Quran Pro includes Apple TV listening in its subscription; Muslim Pro, Quran TV, Quran Radios and Quran+ also ship tvOS builds.

How we compared

Third-party App Store listings were checked on 17 October 2026 in the US App Store, and Sajdah's own row was updated on 23 November 2026 for version 1.7.4 (Prayer Screen, custom mosque calculation methods, Lifetime Premium and scenic backgrounds). An app appears here only if its App Store page lists an Apple TV entry with a tvOS version under "Compatibility".

This comparison focuses strictly on the TV experience from the sofa with the Siri Remote: listening, following the Quran text, Prayer Screen / prayer times and Athan, live streams, Top Shelf, ads on the TV, offline support, and price.

For an app built only for Apple TV, everything on its store page describes the TV app. For a phone app with a TV version, most of the listing describes the iPhone app, so I count a feature only if the listing confirms it works on Apple TV; otherwise the table marks "Not stated for Apple TV". I left out App Store ratings because combined iPhone+TV ratings do not measure the TV experience.

The apps, one by one

Sajdah (built only for Apple TV)

Sajdah is my app, designed exclusively for tvOS and the Siri Remote. Every reciter (200+), synchronized Arabic verse tracking, one translation, surah repeat, sleep timer, Today dashboard, Khatmah tracker, and morning/evening Adhkar are free with zero ads. Optional Sajdah Premium (monthly, yearly, or a one-time Lifetime unlock added in 1.7.3) adds the full-screen Prayer Screen in six styles with OLED burn-in protection, 13 Athan voices, 15 mosque calculation methods with Hanafi Asr and ±30 minute tuning, Qibla direction, Quran Radio and live Makkah TV, word-by-word highlighting, six Quran fonts, six scenic backgrounds, and A-B memorization loops. Every Premium feature can be previewed inside the app (1.7.4).

Weak points: Sajdah streams recitations and live feeds over the internet, so it does not work offline. The full Prayer Screen styles, Athan, and Live TV require Premium.

Quran Mushaf TV (built only for Apple TV)

Quran Mushaf TV displays full Madani Hafs Mushaf pages on the TV, and the developer says scholars reviewed every page. You can read with translation and tafsir, play recitation, repeat a verse and bookmark verses. Crucially, it works offline, which Sajdah does not. Version 3.9.2 (14 October 2026) added the IndoPak Mushaf with auto-scroll. It contains ads, and premium plans start at $0.99/week or $100 lifetime.

the Holy Quran TV! (built only for Apple TV)

A $4.99 paid app featuring one reciter, Sheikh Abdelbasit Abdelsamad, with verse-by-verse recitation. Its store description was written for the iPad version, and its last update was on 4 March 2016.

Quran Pro (phone app with a TV version)

Quran Pro's listing confirms that one subscription covers listening across Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay. If you already pay for Quran Pro on iPhone, it is an easy win to install on your TV too. The listing does not detail the tvOS interface beyond audio playback.

Ameen (phone app with a TV version)

Ameen is a charity (waqf) project — free to download with no in-app purchases listed. Its recitation player supports Apple TV, though the store page does not specify which other phone features carry over to tvOS.

Muslim Pro, Quran TV, Quran Radios and Quran+

These four are iPhone apps that include a confirmed tvOS build, but their App Store descriptions do not specify the TV feature set. Muslim Pro, Quran TV (from the Quran Majeed team) and Quran Radios are free downloads if you want to test them on your living-room TV; Quran+ costs $7.99.

Which app fits which household

  • You want one living-room TV app for the whole family — Quran recitation, verse/word tracking, a full-screen Prayer Screen clock, 13 Athan voices, live Makkah TV, Top Shelf and no ads: Sajdah. (I make it, so weigh that.)
  • You read from traditional Mushaf pages, want tafsir on TV, or need offline access: Quran Mushaf TV. It works offline; Sajdah does not.
  • You want a simple free recitation player with zero purchases: Ameen.
  • You already pay for Quran Pro on your iPhone: its subscription already unlocks listening on Apple TV.
  • You already use Muslim Pro, Quran TV or Quran Radios on your phone: install the TV companion and see if it suits your room.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Quran app for Apple TV?
For an all-in-one living-room app with 200+ reciters, verse and word-by-word sync, a full-screen Prayer Screen in 6 styles, 13 Athan voices, live Makkah TV, Top Shelf and no ads, choose Sajdah. For offline Madani or IndoPak Mushaf reading with tafsir, choose Quran Mushaf TV. For a free charity recitation player with no in-app purchases, try Ameen.
Which Apple TV Quran app works offline?
Quran Mushaf TV works offline for reading the Mushaf pages. Sajdah streams its 200+ reciters and live channels over the internet, so Sajdah requires an active internet connection.
Can I get Athan and a full-screen prayer times display on Apple TV?
Yes. Sajdah includes the Prayer Screen (six full-screen masjid-style displays with OLED burn-in protection and Arabic RTL layout), 13 Athan voices, Qibla direction, and 15 calculation methods with custom city and ±30 minute adjustments.
Which Quran apps on Apple TV have no ads?
Sajdah has zero ads (free to download with optional monthly, yearly or one-time Lifetime Premium). Ameen is a free waqf project with no in-app purchases listed. Quran Mushaf TV is marked "Contains Advertising" on the App Store.
Do Quran.com, Tarteel or Quran Majeed have an Apple TV app?
As checked on the US App Store, Quran by Quran.com, Tarteel, Quran Majeed, Ayah, iQuran and MP3Quran do not list a tvOS compatibility entry. Muslim Pro and Quran Pro do include an Apple TV version alongside their phone apps.
