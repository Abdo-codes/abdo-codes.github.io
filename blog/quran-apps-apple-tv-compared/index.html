The best Quran apps for Apple TV, compared (2026)

I found nine Quran apps that run on Apple TV. Here is what each one offers, what it costs, and who it suits. Every row has a source.

Abdelrahman Eita · 17 October 2026 · 8 min read

Disclosure first: I make Sajdah, one of the apps in this list. I tried to be fair. Where another app is stronger, I say so. You can check every claim in the sources.

In short

  • Sajdah is built only for Apple TV. It puts recitation, prayer times with Athan, live TV and Top Shelf in one app. It needs the internet.
  • Quran Pro and Muslim Pro are big phone apps that also run on Apple TV. They have the most ratings. Both contain ads unless you pay.
  • Ameen is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases listed.
  • Quran Mushaf TV shows the Madani Mushaf page on your TV and works offline.
  • Quran Radios streams more than 1,000 Quran radio stations.
  • Quran TV by the Quran Majeed team has 53 text translations and 22 interface languages.

How we compared

I checked every app on 17 October 2026 in the US App Store. An app is in this list only if its App Store page shows an Apple TV entry with a tvOS version under "Compatibility".

For each app I recorded the price, reciters, translations, interface languages, highlighting, prayer times, live radio or TV, Top Shelf, offline use, ads, the last update and the rating. I used the App Store page and the developer's own text. I did not install and test each app on an Apple TV.

Many of these apps also run on iPhone, and their store text describes the whole app. It rarely says which features work on the TV. In the table, a star (*) means the listing names the feature but does not say it works on Apple TV. "Not stated" means I found no claim either way. Ratings are US ratings and include all devices.

The apps, one by one

Sajdah

Sajdah is my app. It runs only on Apple TV, so every screen is built for the Siri Remote. It has 200+ reciters, verse sync for free, and word-by-word highlighting on selected reciters with Premium. Premium also adds prayer times with Athan, Qibla, Quran radio and live Islamic TV. Top Shelf shows where you stopped, the verse of the day and the next prayer. The release notes list what is free.

Weak points: Sajdah needs the internet, because it streams. It is new, so it has no rating yet. It runs only on Apple TV, so it does not follow you to your phone.

Quran Pro

Quran Pro is a large, well-rated app (4.8 from 109K ratings). It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision, Apple Watch and Apple TV. Its listing says one subscription covers listening on Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay. It offers downloads for offline listening, playlists and several fonts. The page says it contains ads. It does not describe the Apple TV screens.

Muslim Pro

Muslim Pro has the most ratings in this list (600K) and 22 interface languages. It covers prayer times with Athan alerts, Quran reading and listening, Qibla, duas and a prayer tracker. Premium removes ads and adds offline Quran text and audio. The listing names Apple TV as a device but does not say which features are on the TV.

Ameen

Ameen is a charity (waqf) project. It is free, its description says "no ads", and the page lists no in-app purchases. Its recitation player supports Apple TV. It also offers tafsir, prayer times with Adhan, Qibla and adhkar. Version 1.0.9 notes mention live streams from Makkah and Madinah. If cost matters most, start here.

Quran TV (Muslims & Islam)

This app comes from the team behind Quran Majeed. It shows animated Quran text over HD background videos. It names 15 reciters, offers 53 text translations and audio translations in English and Urdu, and supports the IndoPak font. Its interface comes in 22 languages, the widest in this list with Muslim Pro. It contains ads.

Quran Mushaf TV

Quran Mushaf TV is built only for Apple TV. It shows the Madani Hafs Mushaf page, and the developer says scholars reviewed every page. It works offline, and it was updated three days before this check. Recent versions added recitation, verse repetition, translation, tafsir and bookmarks. It contains ads, and premium plans start at $0.99 a week.

Quran Radios

Quran Radios is the choice for radio. It lists more than 1,000 Quran stations and a library of over 500,000 audio files. You can record broadcasts and play the recordings offline. It also lists prayer times and Qibla. It contains ads, which a $3.99 purchase removes.

Quran+ and the Holy Quran TV!

Quran+ is a $7.99 app with Quran, hadith, translations, prayer times and Qibla. Its description is mostly in Indonesian. The Holy Quran TV! is a $4.99 app for Apple TV with one reciter, Sheikh Abdelbasit Abdelsamad. Its last update was in March 2016.

Which app fits which person

  • You want one TV app for the whole home, with recitation, prayer times, Athan, live Makkah TV and Top Shelf: Sajdah. (I make it, so weigh that.)
  • You already use Quran Pro or Muslim Pro on your phone: install it on the TV too. Quran Pro says one subscription covers Apple TV.
  • You want it free with no ads: Ameen.
  • You read from the Mushaf page, or your internet is weak: Quran Mushaf TV works offline. Sajdah does not.
  • You mostly listen to Quran radio: Quran Radios, for its 1,000+ stations.
  • You need many translations: Quran TV, with 53 text translations.

Apps I checked but left out

These apps have no tvOS entry on their App Store page, so they are not in the table: Quran Majeed, Quran by Quran.com, Al Quran (Tafsir & by Word), iQuran, Ayah, MP3Quran and Tarteel. The release notes of Quran Audio by Quran.com mention Apple TV support, but its page lists no tvOS version, so I could not confirm it. Al Quran Tv appears in web search results, but its App Store page did not load in the US, UK, Saudi, UAE, Tunisia or France stores.
