Dinosaur coloring app for kids: ColorTalk 1.2 and Arabic

Version 1.2 adds a Dinosaurs book with 20 dinosaurs to color and a valley to explore, and an Arabic letters book with 28 letters to color and trace. It has been on the App Store since 16 November.

Abdelrahman Eita · 19 November 2026 · 4 min read

ColorTalk is a calm coloring app for iPad, for children ages 2 to 5. Version 1.2 has been on the App Store since 16 November. It adds two new books, so the Home shelf now has four: Letters, Numbers, Arabic Letters and Dinosaurs.

  • A dinosaur coloring book with Dino Valley
  • Arabic letters to color and trace
  • Trace the letter, then the word
  • Color by number for 3, 4 and 5
  • Glow, My Art frames and a snowman
  • Easier to tap, and more app languages
  • Questions

A dinosaur coloring book with Dino Valley

The new Dinosaurs book has 20 dinosaurs to color. When your child colors dinosaurs, a new part of Dino Valley opens. In the valley, your child:

  1. Follows the tracks.
  2. Finds the dinosaurs.
  3. Says hello.

The Dinosaurs book has no voice. If the app screens are in Arabic, the dinosaur names and facts are now in Arabic too.

Arabic letters to color and trace

The new Arabic Letters book has the 28 Arabic letters. Each letter has a page to color. Your child can then trace the letter and the word. This book has no voice, so it works well for quiet time.

كتاب الحروف العربية يضم ثمانية وعشرين حرفًا. يلوّن طفلك صفحة كل حرف، ثم يتتبّع الحرف والكلمة بإصبعه.

Trace the letter, then the word, then color

Tracing is now a clear order. Your child traces the letter first, then the word, then colors the page. This is true for the letters in the ABC book and in the Arabic book.

Color by number for 3, 4 and 5

The Numbers book has new color-by-number pages for 3, 4 and 5. They add to the pages from 1 to 10 that came in version 1.1.

Glow, My Art frames and a snowman

  • Glow. This new tool makes colors glow.
  • My Art frames. Your child picks a frame and hangs each finished picture in it.
  • A snowman page. It is a new page to color.

Easier to tap, and more app languages

  • Bigger targets. Buttons and color swatches are easier to tap.
  • VoiceOver and large text. Support is better.
  • More languages. The app screens now also come in British English, Arabic, German and French. The ABC book, its words and Lumo's voice stay in English.

Still no ads, no accounts and no purchases

ColorTalk 1.2 has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases. The privacy policy lists what the app sends.

Questions

Is there a dinosaur coloring app for kids on iPad? Yes. ColorTalk 1.2 has a Dinosaurs book with 20 dinosaurs to color. After the coloring, your child explores Dino Valley, follows the tracks and says hello to dinosaurs.

Can my child trace Arabic letters in ColorTalk? Yes. The Arabic letters book has the 28 Arabic letters. Each letter has a page to color. Your child traces the letter, then the word.

Does ColorTalk have color by number? Yes. ColorTalk 1.2 has new color-by-number pages for the numbers 3, 4 and 5.

Is ColorTalk free? Yes. ColorTalk is free on the App Store for iPad. It has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

Does the Arabic letters book have a voice? No. The Arabic letters book and the Dinosaurs book have no voice.

Get the update

ColorTalk 1.2 is free on the App Store for iPad. Update or download it on the App Store. New to ColorTalk? Read the launch post. If you have an idea for a page, write to me through ColorTalk support.
