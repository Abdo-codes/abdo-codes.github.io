Release notes · Angry Nona: Night Shift for iPhone

Angry Nona 1.7: faster first nights, title shop hints & polish

Version 1.7 smooths out the first five minutes in the nursery. Night 1 is now a one-minute shh-only shift, Nights 2 and 3 run 75 seconds each, and the title screen tells you when your earned points can unlock something in the Nanny shop.

Abdelrahman Eita · 26 November 2026 · 3 min read

Angry Nona: Night Shift is an iPhone nanny game where one nanny keeps six toddlers asleep until dawn. Version 1.7 is live on the App Store now, in English and Egyptian Arabic (سكّت النونة). This update focuses on the opening minutes, clearer shop progress, and audio and accessibility polish.

In short

  • A 60-second shh-only Night 1 that starts right away with no Tonight card wait.
  • Shorter Nights 2 and 3 at 75 seconds each, with focused first-clear win cards.
  • Nanny shop hints on the title screen showing what your earned points can already buy.
  • Cleaner audio, HUD, haptics & accessibility for VoiceOver, Reduce Motion and large text.
  • Free vs. Full Shift stays simple: Nights 1 to 4 remain free, with one optional Full Shift unlock.

Night 1 is now a 60-second first shift

Early analytics showed players dropping off before the first stir even happened. In 1.7, the opening scene shows its skip chip from the first frame, and your very first attempt on Night 1 launches straight into the room without stopping on a Tonight card.

The first toddler stirs three to four seconds in, never starting with Princess Nona, and Night 1 has no item requests yet. You just learn to tap, shh, and stop one crier from waking the room, all inside a brisk 60 seconds.

Faster Nights 2 and 3, quieter prompts

Nights 2 (Bottle Run) and 3 (Full Row) now last 75 seconds instead of 100 seconds, with Night 3's combo pin tuned from 13 down to 10 to fit the tighter clock. Your first clear on Nights 1 to 3 shows a cleaner win card focused on Next night and Menu.

The optional Mom's text daily reminder now waits until you clear Night 2 and only appears on the title screen, so it never covers a Tonight card or steals a Start tap.

Nanny shop hints right from the title screen

Points come only from playing shifts, and many players piled them up without noticing what they could unlock. The Nanny shop button on the title screen now flags when your balance is ready to grab an upgrade or outfit.

Audio, HUD, haptics & accessibility polish

  • Shift audio & haptics: tighter sound transitions across shifts, paused overlays and HUD pills, with gated UI haptics that respect your settings.
  • VoiceOver & Reduce Motion: refined screen-reader isolation on overlays, banners and title controls, plus calmer motion transitions.
  • Large text (AX3) & Settings: the Daily Night six-crib grid, summary card and Settings rows (full language pill label and stacked Mom's text row) now fit cleanly at accessibility text sizes in both English and Arabic.

What is free, and what Full Shift unlocks

Angry Nona: Night Shift is free to download on iPhone (iOS 18 or later) with no ads and no tracking. Nights 1 to 4 are free. Full Shift is a single one-time purchase (Family Shareable, no subscription) that unlocks Nights 5 to 25, Endless mode, and the Dawn medal. Version 1.7 also adds a one-time Full Shift introduction after winning Night 1 alongside a shortcut on the title screen; shop points are never sold for real money.

Get version 1.7

Angry Nona: Night Shift 1.7 is on the App Store now. Download or update on the App Store. Catch up on daily streaks in the 1.6 release notes, or reach out through Angry Nona support if anything feels off.
