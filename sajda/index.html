Frequently Asked Questions

Sajdah is a Quran app for Apple TV featuring 200+ reciters with verse-by-verse sync, live Islamic TV channels, Quran radio stations, a sleep timer, A-B repeat for memorization, prayer times with 13 athan voices, Qibla direction, playback speed control, focus mode, translations, and stunning animated backgrounds, all designed for your big screen.

Version 1.5.0 brings Radio and Live TV together in one Live tab, adds Quran fonts, word-by-word recitation tracking on selected reciters, a clearer player, Top Shelf cards on the Apple TV Home Screen, and the verse of the day in your app language.

Sajdah is free to download. Every reciter, the synchronized Arabic text, one translation, surah repeat and the sleep timer are free. Sajdah Premium adds Quran radio, live TV, prayer times with Athan, Qibla direction, Prayer Room, ambient mode, all translations and transliteration, word-by-word highlighting, memorization tools, playback speed and extra Quran fonts.

Sajdah offers over 200 Quran reciters with different narrations including Hafs, Warsh, and others. Popular reciters include Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Abdul Rahman Al-Sudais, Maher Al Muaiqly, and many more.

Yes, Sajdah includes accurate prayer times based on your location with 13 beautiful athan voices to choose from. Get alerts for all five daily prayers and see Qibla direction on your Apple TV screen.

Sajdah supports 11 languages: English, Arabic, Turkish, French, Spanish, Urdu, Bengali, Indonesian, Russian, Swedish, and Chinese.

Sajdah is available on Apple TV (tvOS). It is designed specifically for the big screen experience, making it perfect for listening to Quran in your living room.

Download Sajdah from the App Store on your Apple TV. Browse over 200 reciters, select any surah, and listen with verse-by-verse sync on your big screen. You can also use Play All or Shuffle to listen continuously.

Yes, Sajdah displays accurate prayer times based on your location and plays the athan on your Apple TV. It supports all five daily prayers, Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha, with customizable alerts.

The easiest way to play Quran on your TV is through Sajdah on Apple TV. It streams Quran recitations from 200+ reciters with beautiful backgrounds and verse tracking.

Yes, Sajdah offers live Islamic TV channels and Quran radio stations with a live equalizer. These are premium features available through in-app purchase.

Yes! Sajdah has A-B repeat that lets you loop specific verses for Hifz practice. Combined with playback speed control (0.5x to 2x), repeat modes, and focus mode for distraction-free verse display, it's a powerful tool for memorization.

Sajdah streams Quran recitations over the internet for the best quality and widest reciter selection. An internet connection is required to listen to recitations and get prayer times.
