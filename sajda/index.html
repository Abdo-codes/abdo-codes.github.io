Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sajda?

Sajda is a free Quran app for Apple TV featuring 200+ reciters with verse-by-verse sync, live Islamic TV channels, Quran radio stations, a sleep timer, A-B repeat for memorization, prayer times with 13 athan voices, Qibla direction, playback speed control, focus mode, translations, and stunning animated backgrounds — all designed for your big screen.

Is Sajda free?

Sajda is free to download. Quran listening with 200+ reciters, verse-by-verse sync, search, favorites, and playback controls are completely free. Premium features including live TV channels, radio stations, prayer times, athan alerts, and premium backgrounds are available through affordable in-app purchases.

What reciters are available?

Sajda offers over 200 Quran reciters with different narrations including Hafs, Warsh, and others. Popular reciters include Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Abdul Rahman Al-Sudais, Maher Al Muaiqly, and many more.

Does Sajda have prayer times and athan?

Yes, Sajda includes accurate prayer times based on your location with 13 beautiful athan voices to choose from. Get alerts for all five daily prayers and see Qibla direction on your Apple TV screen.

What languages does Sajda support?

Sajda supports 11 languages: English, Arabic, Turkish, French, Spanish, Urdu, Bengali, Indonesian, Russian, Swedish, and Chinese.

What devices can run Sajda?

Sajda is available on Apple TV (tvOS). It is designed specifically for the big screen experience, making it perfect for listening to Quran in your living room.

How to listen to Quran on Apple TV?

Download Sajda from the App Store on your Apple TV. Once installed, browse over 200 reciters, select any surah, and listen with verse-by-verse sync on your big screen. You can also use Play All or Shuffle to listen continuously.

What is the best Quran app for Apple TV?

Sajda is the most feature-rich Quran app for Apple TV — 200+ reciters, verse-by-verse sync, live Islamic TV channels, radio stations, sleep timer, A-B repeat for memorization, prayer times with 13 athan voices, Qibla direction, focus mode, translations, playback speed control, and beautiful themes — all on your big screen.

Can I get prayer times and athan on Apple TV?

Yes, Sajda displays accurate prayer times based on your location and plays the athan on your Apple TV. It supports all five daily prayers — Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha — with customizable alerts.

How to play Quran on TV?

The easiest way to play Quran on your TV is through Sajda on Apple TV. It streams Quran recitations from 200+ reciters with beautiful backgrounds and verse tracking, turning your living room into a peaceful Quran experience.

Does Sajda have live TV and radio?

Yes, Sajda offers live Islamic TV channels and Quran radio stations with a live equalizer. These are premium features available through in-app purchase.

Can I use Sajda for Quran memorization?

Yes! Sajda has A-B repeat that lets you loop specific verses for Hifz practice. Combined with playback speed control (0.5x–2x), repeat modes, and focus mode for distraction-free verse display, it's a powerful tool for memorization.

Does Sajda work offline?

Sajda streams Quran recitations over the internet for the best quality and widest reciter selection. An internet connection is required to listen to recitations and get prayer times.
