Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Sajdah ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use Sajdah on Apple TV.

Information We Collect

Sajdah collects limited diagnostics and usage analytics to keep the app reliable and improve features. This may include app version, device model, language, country or region, screen views, feature events such as playback start and search, crash diagnostics, purchase and subscription events, and approximate session activity. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, messages, or precise location for analytics. We do not sell your data.

Website Analytics

On this website, Google Analytics measures usage such as page views and visit duration.

Location and Prayer Times

If you grant location permission, your device location is used to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. Location is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Data Stored on Your Device

Sajdah stores app preferences such as accent color, appearance, and language on your Apple TV. It also stores recently played Surahs, playback positions, and downloaded background videos on the device.

Third-Party Services

Sajdah uses Firebase/Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Amplitude, StoreKit/App Store services, and Quran audio or content providers. These services may process the limited diagnostics, analytics, purchase, crash, or content-request data needed to operate and improve the app.

Children's Privacy

Sajdah is for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
