Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sajdah free?

Sajdah is free to download with zero ads. All 200+ Quran reciters, verse-by-verse sync, one translation, all five daily prayer times, the dynamic living-sky Horizon Prayer Screen with Quick Control Dock, Ramadan Living Room Mode (Iftar and Suhoor countdowns, Iftar dua, 30-day Khatmah, and Hijri date adjustment), Family Hifz across all 114 surahs, playing starter and saved playlists, Juz 1–30 jump, Quick Tafsir (Al-Muyassar), Morning and Evening Adhkar with Auto-Pace, the sleep timer, and a 60-second daily preview of Quran Radio and Live TV are free. Optional Sajdah Premium (monthly, yearly, or a one-time Lifetime purchase) unlocks creating and editing custom playlists, 13 Athan voices, Qibla direction, 15 calculation methods and Iqamah offsets, all 11 Prayer Screen styles, unlimited Quran Radio and Live TV, Ambient mode, word-by-word highlighting, all translations, memorization repeat and speed controls, and extra Quran fonts.

Do I need an iPhone or AirPlay to use Sajdah on Apple TV?

No. Sajdah is built natively for Apple TV (tvOS 17 or later) and runs directly on Apple TV 4K and Apple TV HD with your Siri Remote. Prayer times, Ramadan Iftar and Suhoor countdowns, Athan alerts, live Makkah TV, and Quran recitations all work natively on your TV without AirPlay or screen mirroring.

How do I set up prayer times and adjust the Hijri date for Ramadan?

Allow location permission on Apple TV to see all five daily prayer times and the free Horizon Prayer Screen automatically. In Settings → Prayer Times, everyone can adjust the Hijri date by −2 to +2 days to match local moon sighting. Sajdah Premium also lets you pick any city manually, choose among 15 calculation methods (including ISNA, MWL, Umm al-Qura, Egyptian, Diyanet, and Standard or Hanafi Asr), set high-latitude short-night rules, tune each prayer by up to ±30 minutes, configure Iqamah countdowns, and pick from 13 Athan voices.

How do I change the reciter or browse by Juz?

Open the Reciters tab to browse 200+ reciters with Hafs, Warsh, and other narrations, or use Search to find reciters and surahs by Arabic name, English name, meaning, or number. Inside any reciter's surah list, use the Juz 1–30 filter rail to jump straight to any Juz.

Can I use Sajdah for Quran memorization and Family Hifz?

Yes. Family Hifz is free on the Today tab and guides the whole family through any of the 114 surahs with listen-and-repeat recitation. Sajdah Premium adds single-verse repeat, A-B repeat, playback speed control (0.5x to 2x), and word-by-word highlighting for focused Hifz practice.

How do I restore my Sajdah Premium subscription or Lifetime purchase?

Make sure your Apple TV is signed in to the same Apple Account used for the purchase, then open Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases inside Sajdah. Monthly, yearly, and one-time Lifetime purchases restore automatically.

What languages does Sajdah support?

Sajdah supports 11 languages: English, Arabic, Turkish, French, Spanish, Urdu, Bengali, Indonesian, Russian, Swedish, and Chinese. Sajdah follows your Apple TV language automatically, and you can switch languages anytime in Settings.

Does Sajdah work offline?

Sajdah streams Quran recitations, Quran Radio, and Live Islamic TV over the internet for the best audio quality and widest reciter selection. An internet connection is required for streaming.

Getting Started on Apple TV

1. Open the App Store on your Apple TV and search for Sajdah (free, no account needed).

2. Allow location permission to see today's five daily prayer times, Iftar/Suhoor countdowns, and the free Horizon Prayer Screen.

3. Pick any of 200+ reciters, start a ready-made playlist like Friday Al-Kahf, or open Family Hifz from Today.
