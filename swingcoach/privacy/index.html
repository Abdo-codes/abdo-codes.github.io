SwingCoach / Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Overview

SwingCoach counts your kettlebell swings and gives form cues with your iPhone camera. The app does its analysis on your devices. It has no account, records no video, and does not upload camera images, body measurements, or your workout history. From version 1.1, the iPhone app sends crash reports and usage analytics to Google Firebase. “Crash Reports and Analytics” below tells you what they contain. Version 1.0 sends neither.

Camera

SwingCoach uses the camera only while you set up, do the five-swing check, or train. Apple Vision finds your body position on the iPhone.

  • The app does not record video.
  • Camera frames and body measurements are not uploaded.
  • We do not see your camera image at any time.

Data Stored on Your iPhone

SwingCoach keeps these items in its local storage on your iPhone:

  • Your answers to the setup questions and your starter plan.
  • Completed workouts: rep counts, ratings, and form trends for History.
  • Rep snapshots, if you turn on Keep key rep snapshots. These are still images of the deepest hinge and the final lockout of a rep, with the measured angles. They are not video.
  • Your training schedule and reminder times.

This data does not leave your device, except through your own iPhone or iCloud backups. We do not have access to it. The broad usage events under “Crash Reports and Analytics” are the only exception.

Apple Watch and Apple Health

The Apple Watch app is optional. If you use it, the Watch asks for Health access to:

  • Read your heart rate and active energy during a workout.
  • Save the workout to Apple Health as a Functional Strength Training workout.

The Watch can also use wrist motion during an active workout to help with swing timing. The Watch and the iPhone send workout state, controls, and these values to each other directly. Health and motion data stay on your devices and in Apple Health. We never receive them, and they are not used for advertising.

Notifications

If you turn on training reminders, the iPhone schedules them on the device. No server sends them.

Crash Reports and Analytics

Version 1.0 sends no crash reports or usage analytics. From version 1.1, the iPhone app uses two Google Firebase services. The Apple Watch app does not use them. Google processes this data for us, and it can process the data in the United States and other countries.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes or records an error, it sends:

  • The stack trace and the state of the app and device at that time.
  • The device model, the operating system version, and the app version.
  • An error code, for errors that do not crash the app.
  • Random IDs for this installation of the app.

We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs. Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days.

Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase). The app sends:

  • The screens that you open.
  • When a workout starts and ends, its type, and whether you completed the ladder.
  • Your progress through setup and the five-swing check. This includes the level that you selected (beginner or experienced), and whether you allowed camera access.
  • Automatic events from Google’s software, such as first open, app sessions, and app updates.
  • The device model, the operating system version, and the language.
  • An approximate location, such as your country or city, that Google gets from your IP address.
  • Random IDs: an app instance ID, a Firebase installation ID, and Apple’s identifier for vendors (IDFV).

We use analytics to learn which parts of the app people use and where setup fails. We keep this data for a maximum of 14 months.

What the app never sends to Firebase. Camera images, rep snapshots, body positions, form measurements, coaching cues, rep counts, and Health or motion data.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, analytics stays off until you tap Allow. Everywhere, you can turn analytics off in Settings. Crash reports stay on for all users, because we need them to keep the app working. In those regions, we rely on your consent for analytics and on our legitimate interest for crash reports.

The app has no account, so we cannot connect these reports to your name. You can email us with a question or a request about your data.

Other packages. The app contains the TelemetryDeck analytics package, but it is not set up in this version, so it sends nothing. If we turn it on, we will update this policy first.

Purchases

SwingCoach is free and has no in-app purchases. The app contains the RevenueCat purchases package, but it is not set up in this version, so it sends nothing.

No Accounts, Ads, or Cross-App Tracking

SwingCoach has no sign-up or login. It shows no ads. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and it does not track you across apps or websites. Ad personalization signals are off, and we share no app data with advertising networks.

Deleting Your Data

To delete all SwingCoach data from your iPhone, delete the app. To remove workouts that the Watch saved to Apple Health, open the Health app. To stop Health access, open the Health app or the Settings app. Reports that the app already sent stay with Google until the times above end. Deleting the app removes its random IDs from your iPhone.

This Website

This policy is about the SwingCoach app. The yoloabdo.com website uses Google Analytics to count page visits. That is separate from the analytics in the app.

Children's Privacy

SwingCoach is not made for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will post the changes on this page with a new date.
