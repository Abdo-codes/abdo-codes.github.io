What it does

A coach that watches every rep.

Built for two-hand and one-arm Russian swings, goblet squats, and kettlebell deadlifts — plus programs, EMOMs, and Apple Watch.

01

Four movements & rep count

Count two-hand swings, one-arm swings (left/right splits), goblet squats, and kettlebell deadlifts hands-free. Adjust the count anytime.

02

Form cues you can hear

When a fault repeats, SwingCoach speaks a short cue:

  • Hinge more, bend your knees less
  • Keep your arms long
  • Finish tall at the top
03

Five-swing check

Do five comfortable swings. You get one clear place to focus and a three-session starter plan. The last session repeats the check, so you can compare.

04

Programs, Plan tab & EMOMs

Schedule weeks ahead, follow the 10,000-Swing Challenge, Bell-Step Progression, or 4-Week Hinge Builder, or run custom EMOM, interval, and cluster timers.

05

Rep review & opt-in video

Inspect joint angles, session pacing, and consistency. Optionally turn on silent local workout video to tap any rep bar and jump straight to that moment.

06

Watch standalone & assist

Count swings directly on Apple Watch without the camera, bridge brief camera drops up to 2.5 s, pace Heart-Rate Guided Rest, and save to Apple Health.

How it works

Set it down. Step back. Swing.

  1. Set up the iPhone

    Set your iPhone or iPad at hip height, 2–3 m (7–10 ft) away, stand side-on with your full body in view — or train standalone on Apple Watch.

  2. Swing

    SwingCoach counts each swing and speaks a cue when a fault repeats. Fix the count with +1 or −1 at any time.

  3. Review

    Swipe through your latest reps, rate how the session felt, and see your progress in History.

Your camera stays private

Pose analysis stays on your device.

Camera pose analysis runs locally with Apple Vision, and optional silent workout video stays on device. Back up privately with optional iCloud Sync. Crash reports and opt-in usage analytics never include camera images or rep counts.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an Apple Watch?
No. Your iPhone or iPad camera counts reps and gives form estimates on its own. With an Apple Watch (watchOS 10 or later), you can also train standalone on your wrist or pair with iPhone for Watch motion assist, Heart-Rate Guided Rest, Double Tap controls, heart rate, calories, and Apple Health sync.
Does SwingCoach record or upload video?
Recording is off by default. Turn on silent local Workout video in Settings to tap any rep bar and jump straight to that moment on your device. SwingCoach never uploads camera frames or videos.
Which exercises does SwingCoach count?
SwingCoach counts and gives form estimates for two-hand Russian swings, one-arm swings (alternating or single-hand), goblet squats, and kettlebell deadlifts.
Which devices does SwingCoach support?
SwingCoach runs on iPhone (iOS 17 or later), iPad (iPadOS 17 or later, portrait and landscape), and Apple Watch (watchOS 10 or later).
