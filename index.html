About

I grew up tinkering — taking things apart to see how they worked, then building something new from the pieces. These days I live in Amsterdam, where I split my time between building apps, 3D printing odd little objects, and a small garden that survives me, barely.

Professionally, that's meant a decade on the Apple ecosystem — a #1 app on the US App Store, products used by millions, years of ecommerce. On my own time, I build things I care about. Sajda brings Quran recitations and prayer times to Apple TV; Tally turns your TV into a scoreboard for game nights; Capybara Rush is a cozy iPhone reflex game. Wi-Fi QR makes guest Wi-Fi QR codes, Airbnb WiFi signs, and printable password signs for hosts, homes, cafes, offices, and rentals; Tateemi helps parents track child vaccinations on their country's schedule; Stampt frames your photos through real vintage postal stamps.

Otherwise: reading, walking the canals, or starting yet another side project I'm convinced the world needs.
