Why Tateemi?

Vaccination schedules by country

Child vaccination schedules differ from one country to another, but every schedule has important dates that start at birth and continue through the early years of childhood. Tateemi lets you choose the right country, then calculates the dose dates from that country's schedule.

Tracking this many dates by hand is hard for any parent. Tateemi calculates all the dates automatically from your child's birth date, and it marks each dose by its status: completed, upcoming, or overdue, so you know exactly where you are and what comes next.

Common questions

Have a question?

Is Tateemi free?
Yes, Tateemi is completely free. There is no monthly subscription, no ads and no in-app purchases. All features are available for free from the first use.
Does Tateemi support more than one country?
Yes. Tateemi supports several vaccination schedules by country. Choose your child's country and the app calculates the dose dates automatically from the matching schedule.
Is my data safe?
Yes. All your data is stored locally on your device only, in the protected app space. We do not send any data to external servers and we do not track users. The app does not require an account or a sign-in.
My child is older. How do I start?
When you add a child older than two months, the app automatically shows a list of the earlier vaccines for their age so you can mark them quickly. After that, it calculates the upcoming dates automatically.
Does Tateemi work without internet?
Yes. The vaccination schedule, your children's data, the reminders and the medical information are all stored locally on your device and work without any internet connection.
