FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is ColorTalk for?
Children 5 and under. ColorTalk teaches the English alphabet on iPad, one letter and one word per page.
Does it work offline?
Yes. All pages, words, and prompts are in the app, so ColorTalk works with no internet connection.
Does it have ads, accounts, or in-app purchases?
No. ColorTalk has no ads, no accounts, no tracking, no third-party analytics, no in-app purchases, and no external links.
Does the app talk?
Only if you turn it on. Spoken prompts are off by default. Open the Parents area, then Settings, and turn on Spoken Prompts. Lumo the firefly then says the letters, words, and prompts in recorded voice clips.
Which devices are supported?
ColorTalk is an iPad app. It runs in landscape and is in English only. More answers are on the support page.
