Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

ColorTalk is an offline iPad app that teaches children the English alphabet through coloring. The app and this policy are in English only. This policy is written for parents and caregivers. The short version is: ColorTalk keeps your child's progress, profile, and settings on the iPad. It sends no analytics or crash reports.

What We Collect

ColorTalk does not collect personal information from your child. The app does not have accounts, sign-in, cloud sync, remote child content, ads, purchases, social features, or tracking.

ColorTalk does not send the child's nickname or age, progress, drawings, learned words, quiz answers, settings, audio, or transcripts to us or anyone else. It does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the identifier for vendors (IDFV). The app works fully offline.

What Stays on the Device

ColorTalk saves a small amount of data locally so the app can work and remember progress. This data is stored in the app's private on-device storage, including a local JSON progress file and iPad app settings.

Local data may include:

  • Coloring progress, such as page fills, strokes, started pages, completed pages, stars, and play days.
  • Learning progress, such as learned words, results of the bundled counting questions, play time by day, and activity history.
  • The child first name or nickname and age that a parent enters in the Parents area.
  • Settings, such as spoken prompts, sound effects, and voice answers on or off.

This local data stays on the iPad. We cannot view it, use it, sell it, share it, or retrieve it remotely.

If you back up the iPad with iCloud Backup or to a computer, that backup includes this local data, as it does for other apps. ColorTalk does not send it to us.

Microphone and Speech

Counting questions use tap answers. Voice answers are optional and off by default. A parent can turn them on or off in the Parents area, which is behind a grown-up question. ColorTalk asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission only for voice answers.

When the child gives a voice answer, ColorTalk uses Apple's on-device speech recognition and requires on-device recognition. Microphone audio is processed on the iPad only. ColorTalk does not record, store, or transmit microphone audio.

The recognized transcript is used only in the moment to decide whether the child answered the bundled counting question. ColorTalk does not store the transcript. The local progress file may store only the quiz word and whether the answer was correct or incorrect, so the parent dashboard can show learning progress.

If microphone or speech permission is denied, the child can still tap the answer.

No Ads, Tracking, or Third Parties

ColorTalk does not show ads and does not track children across apps or websites.

ColorTalk includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and sends no usage or crash reports to third parties.

ColorTalk does not use advertising SDKs, behavioral profiling, or data brokers. All pages, words, and prompts are in the app.

Data Deletion

Parents can use Reset Progress in Settings to erase coloring progress, completed pages, learned words, quiz history, play time, and stars. Reset Progress does not remove the local child profile or app settings.

Parents can use Delete profile & all data to erase progress, the local child profile, and app settings and return ColorTalk to onboarding. ColorTalk has no server-side account or cloud copy of this data. An older iPad backup can still contain earlier app data until you replace or delete that backup.

Children's Privacy

ColorTalk is designed for children 5 and under and their families. We do not knowingly collect, sell, share, disclose, or use children's personal information. We do not use any data for advertising, tracking, or profiling.

If a parent enters a child's first name or nickname and age in the Parents area, that information is stored locally on the iPad and in the iPad's own backups, if backups are on. It is used only to personalize the app experience on that iPad. The app does not ask the child for a name or age.

This Website

This website, yoloabdo.com, uses Google Analytics to count page views and visit length. This applies to the website only, not to the ColorTalk app. The data is anonymous and is not linked to the app, to your child, or to you.

Changes

If ColorTalk's privacy practices change, we will update this policy before shipping the change. Any future feature that sends more data off the device, uses remote content, adds accounts, adds ads, or shares data with another third party would require a new privacy review and an updated policy.
