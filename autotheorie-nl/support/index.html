Frequently Asked Questions

How are Learn, Practice, and Reading organised?
Learn breaks each driving-theory topic into short lessons. Practice offers quick 10-question sets, topic practice, all questions, and a dedicated queue of mistakes and bookmarked questions. Reading lets you browse and search theory chapters or jump straight into topic practice.
Can I open any lesson without completing earlier ones?
Yes. All lessons across every unit are selectable from the start. Tap any topic in Learn to choose a lesson, or tap the menu button inside a lesson to switch to another lesson in that unit.
Why do some Reading topics say “Practice only”?
Topics without a separate written passage are clearly marked Practice only. Tap Practice This Topic at the bottom of the screen to study those questions with full answer explanations.
How do I change the app language?
Open Settings and choose from English, Nederlands (Dutch), العربية (Arabic), Türkçe (Turkish), Polski (Polish), فارسی (Persian), Español (Spanish), or Русский (Russian). Changes apply immediately to lessons, reading chapters, questions, layout direction, and number formatting.
What happens to my progress if I close the app?
Unfinished Learn or Practice sessions start from the beginning after the app closes or restarts. Completed lessons, XP, streak days, saved mistakes, bookmarks, and your language choice remain saved on your device.
How can I replay onboarding or reset my mistakes?
In Settings, tap Replay Onboarding anytime. Answering a saved mistake correctly in Learn or Practice removes it from Practice Mistakes automatically, and tapping the bookmark icon removes or adds review marks.
Is AutoTheorie NL free and offline?
Yes. AutoTheorie NL 1.0 is free with no account, no ads, and no in-app purchases. All study materials run directly from your device. For data details, see the Privacy Policy.
Which devices are supported?
AutoTheorie NL supports iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 / iPadOS 17 or later.
