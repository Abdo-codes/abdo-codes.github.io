Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Overview

AutoTheorie NL helps you study Dutch driving theory at your own pace on iPhone and iPad. The app works offline using bundled study materials, requires no account, and keeps all study progress and preferences on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

AutoTheorie NL stores only the following information locally on your device using Apple’s standard storage:

  • Study progress: completed lessons, lesson scores, total XP, and study streak dates.
  • Practice review state: question IDs marked for review or saved as mistakes, plus your most recently opened Reading and Practice categories.
  • App preferences: your selected study language and onboarding status/timeline choice.

Answers and question position inside an unfinished Learn or Practice session are kept only in memory while that session is open. If the app closes or restarts, unfinished sessions start from the beginning while your saved progress and preferences remain on your device.

No Accounts, Analytics, Ads, or Tracking

  • AutoTheorie NL has no sign-up or login.
  • The app does not include third-party advertising, crash-reporting, or analytics SDKs.
  • The app does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • The app does not transmit your study data or preferences to our servers or any third-party server.

Device Backups & Apple Diagnostics

Your device backups (such as iCloud or encrypted local backups) and operating-system crash diagnostics are controlled by your iOS/iPadOS settings and Apple’s privacy policies.

Deleting Your Data

You can remove all locally stored AutoTheorie NL progress and preferences at any time by deleting the app from your iPhone or iPad.

Support Emails

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the details you choose to include. We use that information only to respond to your request and investigate reported issues. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

This Website

This privacy policy applies to the AutoTheorie NL iOS/iPadOS app. The yoloabdo.com website uses Google Analytics to measure page visits; that website analytics is separate from the AutoTheorie NL app, which sends no analytics.

Independent App Disclaimer

AutoTheorie NL is an independent study app and is not affiliated with CBR.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy in future app versions, we will publish the revised text on this page with an updated date.
