FAQ

How do I play?

Keep six toddlers asleep from 9 PM until dawn. When a kid stirs, tap the crib. Your nanny walks over to shh them or bring what the bubble shows: a bottle, a teddy, or a story. If the noise meter fills, the night is lost.

You can see the rules again at any time. Open Settings and tap How to play.

What is the tap queue?

You can tap several cribs in a row. Your nanny remembers the order and serves them one by one. More queue slots open as you clear nights. You can also buy extra slots in the Nanny shop.

What can I do when the room is quiet?

Do a chore. Warm a bottle, crochet a teddy, or water the plant. Chores are optional, so you can skip them and still clear the night.

Who is Nona?

Nona is the princess with the crown. She has a short fuse. If she wakes up, the other kids can wake up too, so keep her crib calm.

How many nights are there?

There are 13 nights. Each one adds kids or a new twist. Clear a night to open the next one. Nights 1–4 are free. Full Shift, one purchase, opens Nights 5–13 and Endless. To get it back on a new device, tap Restore purchases in Settings.

How do points and the Nanny shop work?

Each shift adds its score to your points. Open Settings and tap Nanny shop to spend them. You can buy:

  • Helps for the campaign nights, like extra queue slots
  • New outfits and looks for your nanny

Every buy asks you to confirm first. Points are earned in play only. They are not sold for real money.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings with the gear on the title screen or in the pause menu. Tap Reset progress, then confirm. This clears every night, star, and medal. Your Endless best stays.

To remove all game data, delete the app from your iPhone.

Does the game support VoiceOver and Reduce Motion?

Yes. VoiceOver reads the room, the kids' needs, and the controls. When Reduce Motion is on in iOS Settings, the game tones down its motion effects.

You can also turn music, sounds, and haptics on or off in the game's Settings.

Does the game have ads or collect data?

No. There are no ads, no account, and no tracking. The game collects no data. Read the privacy policy for details.
