Angry Nona / Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

The short version

Angry Nona: Night Shift collects no data. It has no ads, no tracking, and no account. Everything the game saves stays on your iPhone.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves a few things on your iPhone so it can remember where you are:

  • Nights you cleared, your stars, and your medals
  • Your best scores, including your Endless best
  • Points you earned in play, and what you bought in the Nanny shop
  • Your nanny's outfit and look
  • Settings for music, sounds, and haptics
  • Which tips and How to play pages you already saw

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. If you delete the app, iOS deletes this data too.

No network

The game does not connect to the internet. It does not send or receive any data while you play.

No tracking and no third-party SDKs

The game has no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Points and the Nanny shop

Points are earned by playing. They are not sold for real money. The Nanny shop runs on your device only.

The one in-app purchase, Full Shift, goes through Apple. Apple handles the payment; Angry Nona never sees your payment details and gets no data about you from it.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or delete on our side.

Accessibility settings

The game reads the system VoiceOver and Reduce Motion settings on your device to adjust how it plays. These settings are not stored or sent anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
