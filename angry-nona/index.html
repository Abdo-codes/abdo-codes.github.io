Angry Nona: Night Shift

Six toddlers. One nanny.

You are on the night shift in a busy nursery. Six toddlers need to sleep until dawn. Shh them when they stir, bring them what they ask for, and do not wake Nona.

Coming soon to the App Store See the game

How a night works

Nine hours. Zero naps.

The shift starts at 9 PM. A toddler stirs, a bubble pops up, and the noise meter starts to climb. Tap a crib and your nanny walks over to help. If the room gets too loud, the night is lost. Keep it calm until dawn and you win.

In the game

Small jobs, big feelings.

Every night adds a little more to juggle. Here is what waits for you in the nursery.

Keep six toddlers asleep

Six cribs, one room, one nanny. Get everyone through the night without a meltdown.

Shh and fetch

Read the bubble. Some kids need a quiet shh. Others want a bottle, a teddy, or a story.

The tap queue

Tap a few cribs in a row. Your nanny remembers the order and serves them one by one.

Chores in quiet moments

When the room is still, warm a bottle, crochet a teddy, or water the plant.

Coffee

It is a long night. A cup of coffee keeps your nanny on her feet.

Mom's calls

Mom always calls at the worst time. Decline fast, or the phone rings in the nursery.

Princess Nona

She has a crown and a grudge. When Nona wakes, the whole room can follow.

13 nights + Endless

Thirteen nights that add kids and new twists. Then Endless, which only gets faster. Nights 1–4 are free; one Full Shift purchase opens the rest.

The Nanny shop

Earn points as you play. Spend them in the Nanny shop. Points are never sold for money.

Outfits

Pick a new look for your nanny before the next shift.

VoiceOver and Reduce Motion

The game reads the room out loud with VoiceOver and calms its effects with Reduce Motion.

No ads. No tracking.

No ads, no account, and no tracking. Your progress stays on your iPhone.
